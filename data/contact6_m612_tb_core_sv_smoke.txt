CONTACT6_M612_TB_CORE (s,v)-SLICE SMOKE

Script:
  code/contact6_m612_tb_core_sv_slice.m

Fixing (s,v), the full fiber product is represented by four equations in
(r,K,M,U).  Before saturation the ideal has dimension 2 from boundary
components.  Saturating by r*K*M*D*(U^2-4v^2)*(N+(b+2)D) leaves a
zero-dimensional ideal.

Equation sizes on the tested slices:

  TB: total degree 5, 12--14 terms
  F1: total degree 4, 19 terms
  F2: total degree 8, 86--96 terms
  F3: total degree 5, 26--30 terms

Conclusion: fixing (s,v) is a viable discovery-oriented algebraic slicing.
It removes any height bound on r, in contrast to the faster fixed-(s,r)
candidate verifier.
