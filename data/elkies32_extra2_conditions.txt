# Elkies N=32 reconstructed family: extra independent 2-torsion condition

The built-in root r gives the 2-torsion class 16D.  To force an independent
2-torsion class, impose f(s)=0 for a rational s != r.

After substituting p=4*Pnum(z,r)/Pden(z,r) and the reconstructed formulas for a,b,c,
let Hextra(z,r,s) be the numerator of f(s).  The extra-root cover is
  C32(z,r)=0, Hextra(z,r,s)=0, s != r,
with the diagonal component s=r removed/saturated, away from boundary denominators
z*r*Pden*disc(f)=0.

Pnum degrees (z,r): (7, 5), terms=46
Pden degrees (z,r): (9, 6), terms=48
Numerator Hextra=f(s) degrees (z,r,s): (18, 12, 5), terms=1234
Diagonal check: Hextra(z,r,r) remainder on division by C32 is 0
Diagonal quotient degrees (z,r): (12, 12), terms=79

Hextra is too large to print in notes; it is regenerated by this script.
The search script tests the saturated condition by exact factorization of f(x) and requiring a root s != r.
