CONTACT6_M612_DUAL_SPLIT_CORE_SEARCH

Necessary discriminant covers for the three distinguished dual-kernel
classes:

  R1 halved => DB*DC is a rational square,
  R2 halved => DC is a rational square,
  R3 halved => DB is a rational square,

where

  DB=(a-3)^2-8*(b+3),
  DC=(b-3)^2-8*(a+3).

The search solves every fixed (b,v) cubic-contact core fiber, retains
M=L^2, applies the union of the three square covers, and then verifies that
the contact class is independent of the built-in order-3 direction before
constructing and exact-testing Richelot neighbors.

An audit found that the first raw run counted the degree-drop fiber `b=-3`,
classified empty ideals as exceptional, omitted the special branch `v=1`,
and applied the cover filter before exact core verification.  The corrected
driver skips `b=-3`, handles `v=1` separately, and verifies smoothness,
contact order, and independence before interpreting a cover survivor.
