# Elkies N=32 family: order-64 halving conditions

For F(x)=sum F_i x^i and P=(0,1), D=P-infinity is divisible by 2 iff
there are m,n and k=+/-1 such that, with
  alpha = (F4 - m^2)/(2*F5),
  beta  = (F3 - 2*m*n - F5*alpha^2)/(2*F5),
the two equations
  K2 = F2 - n^2 - 2*m*k - 2*F5*alpha*beta = 0,
  K1 = F1 - 2*n*k - F5*beta^2 = 0
hold.  Equivalently F(x)-(m*x^2+n*x+k)^2 = F5*x*(x^2+alpha*x+beta)^2.

After the first N=32 rank condition and z=a/p:
  a = z*p
  c = (-z^4*p + 4*z^3*p - 8*z^2*p + 8*z^2 + 8*z*p - 16)/(4*z^2)
  b = (-z^4*p + 8*z^3*p - 12*z^2*p + 4*z^2 + 8*z*p - 16)/(4*z^2)
  F1=2*c, F2=c^2+2*b, F3=2*a+2*b*c, F4=b^2+2*a*c, F5=a*(2*b-a)

On the reconstructed N=32 component, (z,r) additionally satisfies
  C32(z,r)=0, the bidegree (4,4) genus-0 equation in data/elkies32_reconstruct_conditions.txt,
and p is the rational common-root formula recorded there.  Thus the order-64 cover is
  C32(z,r)=0, p=4*Pnum(z,r)/Pden(z,r), K1(z,p,m,n)=K2(z,p,m,n)=0
for one of the two signs k=+/-1, away from the boundary denominators z*p*F5*Pden=0.
