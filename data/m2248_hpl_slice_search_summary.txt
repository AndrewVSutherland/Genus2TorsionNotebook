Conclusion:
  Simple HPL line slices are not producing a usable elliptic fibration. Even after forcing C=c^2 and F1 square by construction, the first remaining square condition D=d^2 has genus at least 2 for all small directions tested. This explains why the numeric line-slice searches find no nontrivial branch-square point. The next algebraic step should not be another line slice; it should use the HPL witness to build a higher-dimensional conic/elliptic fibration that makes at least two of the remaining full-cover square conditions automatic.
