A2244 small-prime bad-reduction congruence diagnostics
surface=(ab+ac+ad+bc+bd+cd)^2-4abcd=0

Bad reduction condition for squared branch coordinates:
  p | a*b*c*d*prod_{i<j}(a_i^2-a_j^2).
For p <= 7 this is automatic for every primitive tuple, since
|(F_p^*)^2|=(p-1)/2 < 4.  Equivalently, modulo p at least
one coordinate is zero or two coordinates are congruent up to sign.

A2244 squareclass condition for a partition I|J:
  all a_i^2-a_j^2, i in I, j in J, have the same Q_p squareclass.
For odd p: same valuation parity and same Legendre symbol of the unit.
For p=2: same valuation parity and same odd unit modulo 8.
Deep means a cross-difference is 0 modulo the tested prime power.
Unit_ambiguous only occurs at p=2 when the tested modulus has not
determined the odd unit modulo 8.
