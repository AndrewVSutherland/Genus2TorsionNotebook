CONTACT7_TWO_ROOT_BOUNDARY

Signed-parameter convention:
  h(1-s^2)=s^7, h(1-t^2)=t^7.

Reduced parameter poles:
  s=-1, t=-1, s=-t.

Let
  H = s^2*t^2 + 2*s^2*t + s^2 + 2*s*t^2 + 2*s*t
      + 1/2*s + t^2 + 1/2*t,

  R = s^4*t^2 + 2*s^4*t + s^4
      + 2*s^3*t^3 + 7*s^3*t^2 + 8*s^3*t + 3*s^3
      + 6*s^2*t^3 + 13*s^2*t^2 + 8*s^2*t + s^2
      + 6*s*t^3 + 7*s*t^2 + 2*s*t + 2*t^3 + t^2.

Then
  h(1) = -s^2*t^2*H / ((s+1)^2*(t+1)^2*(s+t)).

The exact full discriminant factorization is
  Disc_x(f) =
    8*s^14*t^14*(s-t)^2*H^7*R(s,t)^2*R(t,s)^2*K(s,t)
    / ((s+1)^26*(t+1)^26*(s+t)^13),
where K is irreducible, symmetric, and has bidegree (8,8).

Finite singular support:
  s=0, t=0, s=t, H=0, R(s,t)=0, R(t,s)=0, K(s,t)=0.

The exact expanded K and every factorization assertion are produced by:
  magma -b code/contact7_two_root_boundary.m

Magma's raw factor normalization uses R(t,s)/2 and therefore prints raw
scalar 32; the normalization above has scalar 8.
