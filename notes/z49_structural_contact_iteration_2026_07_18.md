# A structural contact-7 route to a rational class of order `49`

## Outcome

Composing the marked order-`7` construction **does** give a compact explicit
order-`49` incidence curve.  It is locally viable: open smooth points over
`F_5` and `F_11` yield divisor classes of exact order `49`.  No rational
point was found, however, in the bounded integral and `p`-adic reconstruction
tests described below.

The corresponding forward iteration of a Pell unit does **not** work.  The
seventh power of a fundamental degree-`7` unit has degree `49`, but is
imprimitive and leaves the infinity divisor at order `7`.

## Iterating the marked divisor

Start with the direct contact-`7` family

```text
h = 1-(7/2)x+a*x^2+b*x^3,
f = (h^2+(x-1)^7)/x^2,
C: y^2=f.
```

For `P=(1,h(1))`, the class `D=P-infinity` has order `7` on the open smooth
family.  Seek a point `R=(r,s)` such that `Q=R-infinity` satisfies
`7Q=+/-D`.  A function with the required poles has the form

```text
psi = (A(x)+B(x)y)/(x-1),
deg(A)=4, deg(B)<=1.
```

Its norm gives the explicit condition

```text
A^2-B^2*f = c*(x-1)*(x-r)^7.                 (1)
```

On the chart where `A` has nonzero leading coefficient, scale `A` to be
monic.  Comparing degree `8` then gives `c=1`.  Write

```text
B=u+v*x,
A=x^4+z3*x^3+z2*x^2+z1*x+z0.
```

The coefficients of degrees `7,6,5,4` in (1) solve successively for
`z3,z2,z1,z0`.  The first two are

```text
z3 = -(7*r-v^2+1)/2,

z2 = (4*b^2*v^2+35*r^2+14*r*v^2+14*r+8*u*v
      -v^4-26*v^2-1)/8.
```

The exact formulas for `z1,z0` are generated by
`code/z49_structural_contact_iterate.py`.  After substitution, the four
remaining coefficients of (1) are four explicit integral equations in

```text
(a,b,u,v,r).
```

Their total degrees and term counts are

```text
degree 16: 379 terms,
degree 14: 236 terms,
degree 12: 142 terms,
degree 10:  81 terms.
```

Thus the point-root construction is an explicit curve: four equations in
five variables.  No Groebner basis is needed to construct or evaluate it.

On the open set

```text
r != 1,
h(1) != 0,
B(1) != 0,
B(r) != 0,
Disc(f) != 0,
```

put `s=-A(r)/B(r)`.  Equation (1) gives `s^2=f(r)`.  Generically one of
`A+By` and `A-By` vanishes to order seven at `R`, while its behavior above
`x=1` selects `P` or its conjugate.  Therefore

```text
7*(R-infinity) = +/-(P-infinity).
```

Since the right side has exact order `7`, `R-infinity` has exact order `49`.

## Finite-field verification

The reduced equations have open smooth solutions over both `F_5` and
`F_11`.  Magma verifies the actual Jacobian relation, not just the norm
identity.  Representatives are

```text
p=5:  (a,b,u,v,r)=(2,1,1,3,0),  #J=49,
p=5:  (a,b,u,v,r)=(2,1,2,2,2),  #J=49,
p=11: (a,b,u,v,r)=(5,7,3,0,7),  #J=147,
p=11: (a,b,u,v,r)=(6,7,4,5,6),  #J=98.
```

In every row Magma gives

```text
Order(D)=7, Order(Q)=49, 7Q=-D.
```

The reduced-incidence Jacobian has rank `4` at these representatives, so
they lie on smooth one-dimensional local branches.  This proves that the
construction has genuine local room and is not a formal identity trapped on
a degenerate component.

## The exhaustive open `3`-adic branches

Modulo `3`, the only open reduced-incidence points are

```text
(a,b,u,v,r)=(1,1,+1,0,0),
(a,b,u,v,r)=(1,1,-1,0,0).
```

The corresponding genus-2 polynomial is singular modulo `3`, so these are
bad-reduction branches rather than finite-field order-`49` examples.  The
incidence Jacobian nevertheless has rank `4`: its `b` column is zero and
the columns `(a,u,v,r)` form an invertible block.  Hence `b` is a valid
`3`-adic local parameter on both branches.

The bounded search enumerated every reduced rational `b` of naive height at
most `200`, denominator prime to `3`, and `b=1 mod 3`.  It lifted the other
four coordinates uniquely to modulus `3^12`, reconstructed them with the
same height bound, and substituted into all four exact equations:

```text
rational b values       12,319
branch lifts            24,638
all four reconstructed       0
exact open hits               0
```

Thus there is no point with all five reduced coordinates of height at most
`200` in either integral open `3`-adic branch.  This does not cover points
nonintegral at `3` or excluded boundary charts.

## Complementary `5`-adic and `11`-adic reconstruction

The finite enumeration gives exactly four open smooth points over `F_5` and
ten over `F_11`.  At every one, the `(a,b,u,v)` Jacobian block is invertible,
so `r` is a local parameter.  All rational `r` of height at most `200` and
denominator prime to the relevant prime were lifted and reconstructed:

```text
p=5:  40,757 rational r, 4 branches, 32,646 compatible lifts,
      2 full coordinate reconstructions, 0 exact hits;

p=11: 44,839 rational r, 10 branches, 40,766 compatible lifts,
      278 full coordinate reconstructions, 0 exact hits.
```

This clears all listed smooth `p`-integral branches at coordinate height
`200`.  It complements, but does not replace, the `3`-adic test: rational
points can be nonintegral at one of these primes or reduce to a boundary.

The initial integral box `|a|,|b|,|u|,|v|,|r|<=3` had `49` solutions of the
raw equations, all on excluded boundary components, and no open solution.

## Why Pell powering does not give order `49`

For

```text
f=x^6+2*x^5-5*x^4-14*x^3-3*x^2+24*x+28,
```

the continued fraction gives fundamental infinity-difference order `7`.
The recovered fundamental Pell unit has degrees `(7,4)` and norm `4/9`.
Raising it to the seventh power produces a valid Pell solution with degrees
`(49,46)` and norm `(4/9)^7`, but the continued-fraction fundamental order
remains `7`.  Thus forward unit composition only produces a seventh power,
not a class of order `49`.

A genuine Pell construction would need a **primitive** unit

```text
alpha^2-f*beta^2 = constant,
deg(alpha)=49, deg(beta)=46,
```

or equivalently a primitive continued-fraction period of total degree `49`.
That is a backward period-engineering problem, not an iteration of the known
order-`7` unit.

## Exact constant-`B` projection and fixed-slope search

The specialization `v=0` can be settled exactly.  Put `w=u^2`, solve the
second residual equation for `a`, and eliminate `w` from the other three.
The two relevant resultants have factor-degree summaries

```text
[(2,1,1), (0,1,5),  (8,12,1)]
[(2,1,1), (0,1,10), (10,13,1)]
```

where each tuple is `(degree in b, degree in r, multiplicity)`.  Eliminating
`b` between the degree-`8` and degree-`10` factors gives a degree-`194`
projection

```text
(r-1)^20 * F_56(r) * F_118(r),
```

with `F_56` and `F_118` irreducible over `Q`.  The only rational linear root
is therefore `r=1`, which is excluded by the open conditions.  The common
conic `2*b^2+7*r-13=0` is a leading-degree artifact and must be checked
separately: direct substitution gives `(b^2-3)^5*P_15` and
`(b^2-3)^10*P_25`, with `gcd(P_15,P_25)=1`; the failed leading coefficient
is irreducible of degree `9`.  Since `b^2-3` has no rational root, the open
`v=0` slice has no rational point.

For nonconstant `B`, fixing the rational slope `T=v/u` keeps the system
zero-dimensional.  Every reduced `T != -1` with numerator and denominator
of absolute height at most `50` was matched against all open `F_5` and
`F_11` branches, Hensel lifted, rationally reconstructed with coordinate
bound `5000`, and substituted into the exact equations:

```text
rational slopes             3,094
nonsingular branch lifts    4,620
singular matches                 0
full reconstructions             2
exact open hits                  0
```

The two reconstructions are the sign-paired `5`-adic approximants at
`T=43/36`; a follow-up lift to `5^30` did not stabilize (three of four
coordinates failed rational reconstruction even at the uniqueness bound).
Thus this is a bounded negative search, not a proof for all `u*v != 0`.

Deep `p^30` probes also found no exact point on the two open `r=0` branches
over `F_5`.  For `u=0`, there are no open points over `F_3`, `F_5`, or
`F_11`; the two open smooth `F_13` points `(8,5,0,3,3)` and
`(8,5,0,10,3)` both failed exact reconstruction after lifting to `13^30`.
These special-slice probes are evidence only; the exact exclusion above is
specific to `v=0`.

The exact projection used about `111 MB` peak RSS and `10.7` seconds.  The
height-`50` fixed-slope search used about `85 MB` and `28.4` seconds; all
new scripts remain comfortably below the memory ceiling.

## Continued nonconstant projection and `3`-adic normalization

On `u*v!=0`, evaluating the norm identity at the rational root `q=-u/v` of
`B` gives the exact square normalization

```text
(q-r)/(q-1)=t^2,       r=q-(q-1)*t^2.
```

For the genuinely nonconstant slope `v/u=1`, the normalized equations have
one small regular component whose final projection is
`t^40*P_248(t)`, with `P_248` irreducible over `Q`; its only rational linear
root is the excluded boundary `t=0`.  A larger resultant component remains
unresolved after reaching the imposed `300 MB` cap.  Finite quotient
diagnostics through `p=23` also show that the natural plane projections have
no equation of total degree at most `3` or bidegree `(2,2)`, `(1,3)`, or
`(3,1)`.

The two integral incidence points modulo `3` were resolved by weighted
blowups into a unique smooth disk on each sign branch.  Six nested
discriminant layers were certified through depth `42`.  A height-`100`
search made `18,206` lifts on these disks with no rational reconstruction;
the root-adapted height-`80` search made `5,927` lifts and likewise found no
exact quotient point.  The balanced first-pole chart remains thick after
three blowups and is not yet a one-dimensional normalization.

## Verdict and next step

The marked-point lift is the viable structural route.  It has explicit
equations, smooth local branches, and certified order-`49` reductions.  The
remaining difficulty is arithmetic: finding a rational point on the
four-equation curve.  Small integral search and all tested integral local
branches through height `200` are empty.

The constant-`B` projection and one regular nonconstant component are now
closed.  The next serious algebraic target is the large `(b-degree 10,
b-degree 12)` normalized component, using modular/saturated projective
elimination rather than the characteristic-zero resultant that hit the
memory cap.  The main local target is normalization of the thick balanced
pole divisor.  A large blind five-variable box is not indicated.
