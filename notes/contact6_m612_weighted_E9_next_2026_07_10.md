## Conclusion

All four surviving `p=5` weighted `E9` points lie on genuine smooth
one-dimensional `Z_5` core branches.  The rank-`4` presentation in the
previous layer was a singular integral model, not a further local
obstruction.

For each of the four points, the `25` first correction digits split into
five orbits under the weighted-scaling tangent.  Exactly five corrections
continue, one in every scaling orbit.  After adjoining the divided fifth
relation, these five points have Jacobian rank `5` in six variables.  Thus
each is a smooth residue disk on a relative one-dimensional core.

The direct exact lift counts for the original singular equations are

```text
modulus                 5     25     125     625
all four base points    4    100     500    2500
growth                       x25      x5      x5
```

More precisely, each base point has `25` solutions modulo `25`, of which
`5` continue modulo `125`; these give `125` solutions modulo `125`, of
which `25` continue modulo `625`; these in turn give `625` solutions modulo
`625`.  The apparently excessive raw solutions are the nilpotent thickness
of the singular model.  The rank-`5` strict transform proves continuation
to all orders.

The exact `R3` halving cover remains compatible.  Both of its smooth
endpoint branches lift through modulus `625` for every `E mod 25` occurring
on the continued core.  Consequently there is no `Q_5` obstruction to the
combined core-plus-`R3` system on this endpoint.  This is still local: it
does not produce a rational point over `Q` on the halving cover.


## The divided-relation blowup

`contact6_m612_weighted_E9_lift.m`.  At one of the four surviving points

```text

Gi(d) = Fi(x0+5*d)/5.
```

unique left relation is

```text

lambda = (0,0,1,3,4)    when E0=4,
```

independently of the sign of `l0`.  The compatibility already proved in
the previous layer implies that


is an integral polynomial.  In the displayed order the coefficient of
`G3` is `1`, so the strict system


is exactly equivalent over `Z_5` to all five original `Gi=0`: after the
four retained equations vanish, `H=0` forces the dropped `G3` to vanish.
The exact `H` has total degree `6` and `69` terms.  The code constructs it


Every consistent right-hand side has `25` correction digits.  The exact

contains smooth local core curves and the only surviving dual class `R3`

and intersect that core curve with the exact rational `R3` cover
