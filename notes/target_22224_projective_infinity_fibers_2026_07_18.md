## Primitive projective masks for P8 and F1--F7

For a repeated fiber `(f1,f2,f3,d0*T^2)` write `T=r/s`.  The signed
branch tuple to reduce is

```text
(f1*s^2, f2*s^2, f3*s^2, d0*r^2),
```

after division by its common local `p`-power.  Reducing the raw tuple
without this division misses three exceptional charts.

The audited table is generated by
`code/target_22224_repeated_fibers_projective_primitive.py` and stored in
`results/target_22224_repeated_fibers_projective_primitive.tsv`.

| fiber | finite `T mod 11` | infinity |
|---|---|---|
| P8 | all classes | all depths |
| F1 | `±1` | all depths |
| F2 | all classes | depth 1: `z=s/(11r)=±1,±2,±4`; depth >=2 allowed |
| F3 | `0,±1,±2,±4` | all depths |

| F5 | `0,±1,±2,±4` | all depths |

The P8 correction is important: at `T=0`, its raw tuple is zero modulo
11, but primitive division gives `(48,-66,-81,0)=(4,0,7,0) mod 11`,
which is a genuine direct-contact boundary class.


## N11: the strongest new denominator fiber

The new repeated fiber can be scaled to

```text
(-960,1800,2535,-1352*T^2),  -1352=-8*13^2.
```

With `T=r/s` and `z=s/(13r)`, the primitive infinity chart is

```text
(-960*z^2,1800*z^2,2535*z^2,-8).
```

The mod-13 direct-contact incidence permits `z=0,±2,±3,±4,±6`.
In particular, the displayed torsion-64 near miss `T=1/13` has `z=1`
and is killed by the exact contact mask.

For nonzero allowed `z`, the unit-`L` witnesses all have contact variable
`v=0`, while `P` is a unit and `v_13(e4)=4`.  The last contact equation
has leading terms `P*v^3-4L^2 e4`, requiring `3 v_13(v)=4`, which is
impossible.  Thus none of these depth-one classes lifts on the unit-`L`
chart.  The cleared `L=0` chart remains separate and was not used for a
global exclusion.

At `z=0`, the witnesses `(L,U,v)=(2,10,4),(11,10,4)` are smooth in the
three contact variables.  Exhaustive lifting through `13^5` gives every
`z in 13 Z_13 / 13^5`, so the denominator-depth-2 lane is genuinely
locally viable.  See `code/target_22224_n11_p13_infinity_lift.py` and its
TSV/log outputs.


gives the exact parameter

```text

```

Modulo `13^2`, the image of the chosen MW basis is


`code/target_22224_n11_p13_mw_mod169.m`.

and 13 and the third-square plus `3 | #J(F_p)` conditions through prime
199, later testing 211, 223, 227, 229, 239, 241, and 251.  Results:
