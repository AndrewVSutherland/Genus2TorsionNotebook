# Target \([2,2,2,24]\): halving the marked order-12 class

Date: 2026-07-18

## Outcome

This attack now has a reproducible global 2-division cover over the direct
\(A(2,2,2,12)\) family, an exact placement of the new
\([2,2,2,12]\) record on that family, and two useful test runs.

* Over the record, the saturated halving fiber has one irreducible
  degree-16 elimination factor.  Magma independently reports that the marked
  order-12 class is not divisible by 2.  Thus this particular record point
  does **not** give \([2,2,2,24]\).
* The transverse \(q=(x+t)^2\) slice is not vacuous: over \(\mathbf F_{29}\)
  it has 96 smooth oriented bases, and the labelled six-class sample contains
  36 marked classes with a half (360 halving points in total).
* The positive rational search of height 20 found 127 double-square points,
  but all lie on the elementary branch-collision component and hence all give
  singular curves.  It found no smooth base and therefore no rational target.

The next sensible computation is a good-prime CRT/Hensel search beginning at
29 and 31, then adding 37 and 41, rather than enlarging the naive rational box.

## 1. Direct \(A(2,2,2,12)\) presentation

Work with

\[
 f=x(x+a^2)(x+b^2)(x+c^2)(x+d^2),\qquad
 q=x^2+ux+t^2,
\]
\[
 h=x^3+\frac{1+3u}{2}x^2+vx+t^3,
 \qquad h^2-f=q^3.
\]

The full rational branch locus gives rational 2-torsion of rank 3.  The
marked classes are

\[
 D_3=[q,h\bmod q],
\]
\[
 g_4=(x-ab)(x-cd)-x(a+b)(c+d),
\]
\[
 L_4=(x-ab)(c+d)+(a+b)(x-cd),
 \qquad D_4=[g_4,-xL_4\bmod g_4],
\]

where \(2D_4=[x,0]\), and \(D_{12}=D_3+D_4\).

To write \(D_{12}\) globally, solve the two linear CRT equations for
\(m_c,n_c\) in

\[
 \ell=(h\bmod q)+q(m_cx+n_c),\qquad
 \ell\equiv -xL_4\pmod {g_4}.
\]

On the open CRT chart,

\[
 \ell^2-f=m_c^2qg_4U,\qquad U=x^2+A_0x+B_0,
\]

and

\[
 D_{12}=[U,V],\qquad V=-\ell\bmod U.
\]

## 2. The global halving cover

For new variables \(M,N\), put

\[
 \ell_H=V+U(Mx+N),\qquad
 S=\frac{\ell_H^2-f}{U}
   =s_4x^4+s_3x^3+s_2x^2+s_1x+s_0.
\]

On the chart \(s_4\ne0\), the quartic \(S\) is a square precisely when

\[
 E_1=8s_4^2s_1-s_3(4s_4s_2-s_3^2)=0,
\]
\[
 E_0=64s_4^3s_0-(4s_4s_2-s_3^2)^2=0.
\]

The corresponding half is

\[
 G=x^2+\frac{s_3}{2s_4}x+
   \frac{4s_4s_2-s_3^2}{8s_4^2},
 \qquad H=[G,-\ell_H\bmod G].
\]

The polynomial incidence presentation uses the variables

```text
a,b,c,d,u,t,v,mc,nc,A0,B0,M,N,s4,s3,s2,s1,s0
```

and consists of four contact equations, two CRT equations, six nonzero
coefficient equations for \(\ell^2-f=m_c^2qg_4U\), seven coefficient
equations for \(\ell_H^2-f=US\), and \(E_1=E_0=0\).  The omitted
\(x^6\)-coefficient in the first identity is identically zero (the earlier
diagnostic degree \(-1\) was only this zero coefficient).  The open condition
is

\[
 \operatorname{disc}(f)\,m_c\,\det_{\rm CRT}(q,g_4)\,s_4\ne0.
\]

The complete expanded equations are in
`results/target_22224_global_cover_equations.txt`.

## 3. Exact recovery of the record

Starting from the roots

\[
 -\frac{75593}{145},\ -519,\ -39,\ -\frac{299}{12},\ 59,\
 \frac{123109}{1740},
\]

send \(-519\) to 0 and \(-39\) to infinity, and normalize the first root to
\(-1\).  After normalizing the contact cubic, the direct coordinates are

\[
 a=\frac{1183}{209760},\qquad
 b=\frac{100793}{209760},\qquad
 c=\frac{41327}{209760},\qquad
 d=\frac{17238221}{91665120},
\]
\[
 u=\frac{289578289}{21999628800},\qquad
 t=\frac{289578289}{43999257600},
\]
\[
 v=\frac{11494656094062061921}{645311556450385920000}.
\]

Magma verifies both \(h^2-f=q^3\) and a \(\mathbf Q\)-isomorphism with the
record curve.  Here \(q=(x+t)^2\).  The marked class is

\[
 U=x^2+\frac{7685474011061}{240345944640000}x+
 \frac{24629228967374764733}{21150086262661398528000000},
\]
\[
 V=\frac{2493860486376464821}{1147465617548904000000}x+
 \frac{15693572293408428464514469}
 {50487635293677307693670400000000}.
\]

Exact Jacobian arithmetic gives

```text
Order(D3), Order(D4), Order(D12) = 3, 4, 12
D12 = D3 + D4
J(Q)_tors = [2,2,2,12]
```

## 4. Fixed-record halving fiber

For the preceding \(U,V\), the two halving equations have degree profiles

```text
E1: total/M/N degrees [6,6,2]
E0: total/M/N degrees [8,8,2]
s4 = M^2
gcd(E1,E0) = 1.
```

Eliminating \(N\) gives a resultant of degree 24 in \(M\).  Its boundary
part is \(M^8\).  After saturation by \(M\), the remaining factor is a single
irreducible factor of degree 16 over \(\mathbf Q\).  In particular the open
halving fiber has no rational point.  On an exact integral model, Magma also
returns

```text
torsion invariants          [2,2,2,12]
order of the marked D12     12
IsDivisibleBy(D12,2)        false
```

Thus both the cover computation and independent Jacobian arithmetic reject
the fixed record as a \([2,2,2,24]\) realization.  As an additional affine
diagnostic, the displayed halving chart has 8 points modulo 29 but none
modulo 31 (and likewise none modulo 37 or 41).  This count is not by itself a
complete p-adic obstruction: a rational point with negative valuation could
reduce to the projective boundary of this affine chart.

## 5. A transverse slice through the record

Set \(q=(x+t)^2\), write \(t=s^2\), and factor

\[
 h-(x+t)^3=\frac{x}{2}(x+d^2),\qquad
 h+(x+t)^3=2(x+a^2)(x+b^2)(x+c^2).
\]

Freely choose \(A,B\), put \(C=(AB)^{-1}\), and impose the two square-cover
conditions

\[
 A^2+B^2+C^2-3=\rho^2,
\qquad
 A^{-2}+B^{-2}+C^{-2}-3=\sigma^2.
\]

Thus \(A,B\) are the free base variables; \(C,\rho,\sigma\) are constrained.
Then

\[
 s=\frac1{2\rho},\quad t=s^2,\quad u=2t,
\]
\[
 (a,b,c)=s(A,B,C),\quad d=2s^2\sigma,\quad
 v=3t^2+\frac{d^2}{2}.
\]

The record lies exactly on this slice at

\[
 s=\frac{17017}{209760},\quad
 (A,B,C)=\left(\frac{13}{187},\frac{77}{13},\frac{17}{7}\right),
\]
\[
 \rho=\frac{104880}{17017},\qquad
 \sigma=\frac{243120}{17017}.
\]

All five displayed relations are checked exactly by the script.

### Why the small-prime statistics were boundary statistics

The record coordinates have poles at every tested prime from 5 through 23:

```text
17017 = 7*11*13*17
209760 = 2^5*3*5*19*23
```

and \(A,B,C\) also have denominators 187, 13, and 7.  Therefore the
`smooth_bases=0` results at \(p\le23\) describe this affine chart's boundary;
they are not evidence against the slice.  Prime 29 is the first useful
positive control in this experiment.

### Finite-field positive control at \(p=29\)

The finite test enumerates all oriented \((A,B)\) satisfying the two square
conditions, then samples the three distinguished cubic-root pairings and the
two signs of \(d\).  It found

```text
double-square oriented bases    276
branch-collision bases          180
smooth oriented bases            96
sampled marked classes          576
marked classes having a half     36
halving (M,N)-points             360
```

This proves that the constructed cover meets the smooth q-square slice and
that the target condition occurs modulo a good affine prime.  It is a search
positive control, not a rational realization.

### Rational height-20 test

The positive reduced-rational box of height 20 contains 255 values.  After
the symmetry ordering \(A\le B\le C\), the search tested 13,420 pairs and
found 127 double-square points.  All 127 are on the elementary component

\[
 \{A,B,C\}=\{r,1,r^{-1}\}.
\]

On this component both displayed expressions are automatically squares, but
the construction has \(d^2=a_i^2\) for one of the three cubic roots.  Hence
\(f\) has a repeated root.  The run consequently found zero smooth bases,
zero marked classes, and zero target hits.  The script now detects and labels
this branch-collision component before entering Jacobian arithmetic.

## 6. Recommended next run

1. Enumerate smooth target points on the labelled cover over 29 and 31,
   retaining \((A,B,\rho,\sigma,M,N)\), not just counts.
2. Match compatible nonsingular residue classes and Hensel-lift them.  Add
   primes 37 and 41 as needed; the product of all four primes is large enough
   for denominator-aware reconstruction at and beyond the record's free
   coordinate height 187.
3. Rationally reconstruct \(A,B\), verify both square equations exactly, and
   reconstruct the direct curve and the marked \(D_{12}\).
4. For every lift, require nonzero discriminant and open-chart factors, then
   verify `Order(D12) eq 12` and `IsDivisibleBy(D12,2)` on an integral model.
5. For a hit, compute the full torsion subgroup and certify geometric
   simplicity using an irreducible Frobenius polynomial/root-power test.

This modular route directly samples the codimension-two halving condition.
It is much better targeted than increasing the naive height box; the first
non-boundary point currently known on this slice has free-coordinate height
187.
