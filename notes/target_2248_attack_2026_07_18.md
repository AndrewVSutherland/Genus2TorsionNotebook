# Target `[2,2,4,8]`: HLP pair fibers and a Richelot attack

Date: 2026-07-18

## Outcome

This bounded attack did **not** find a geometrically simple Jacobian with exact
torsion `[2,2,4,8]`.  It did produce two useful structural conclusions.

1. Each of the three natural simultaneous-pair fibers through the known split
   Howe--Leprévost--Poonen (HLP) point is an elliptic curve of positive rank, but
   the two obvious points differ by rational 2-torsion.  Searching their one
   visible free direction through coefficient bound 12 recovers only the HLP
   point.
2. The entire rational Richelot component of the certified simple `[2,4,8]`
   seed contains 20 geometrically simple Jacobians and no `[2,2,4,8]` node.
   The unique neighbor with full rational 2-torsion has exact torsion
   `[2,2,2,4]`: the extra 2-torsion arrives only after the 8-chain is lost.

Thus the next serious experiment should vary the simple `[2,4,8]` source in
its one-split family and inspect its rational Richelot neighbors.  Continuing
farther on either fixed object below cannot produce a new result: the HLP
search is confined to three fixed elliptic curves, and the seed's rational
Richelot component is closed.

## 1. Three pair fibers through HLP

The normalized HLP point used in `notes/m2248_hpl_and_rst_search.md` is

```text
rho   = 58466134224/53109477625
sigma = 719363573659505664/749082246897952705
tau   = 307598400/352612321
c     = 58466134224/30294861575
d     = 72946054224/53109477625.
```

On the `D=d^2` and `F0=rho*sigma*tau` square surface, write

```text
sigma = q^2*rho/tau.
```

For each of `F1`, `F2`, and `F4`, the remaining square condition has the
shape

```text
y_i^2 = A_i*(1+B_i*q^2).
```

Parametrizing the first conic in a pair by its slope `m` turns the second into
the reciprocal genus-one quartic

```text
W^2 = A2*((m^2-K)^2
          + B2*(q0*m^2-2*y0*m+q0*K)^2),   K=A1*B1,
```

with the HLP point built in.  The script

```text
code/target_2248_hlp_pair_fibers.m
```

constructs all three quartics, minimizes and reduces them, maps bounded
elliptic-curve combinations back to `q`, imposes `C=c^2` and all five cover
square conditions, computes the exact Jacobian torsion, and runs the strict
Frobenius root-power simplicity test for powers 2 through 12.

The arithmetic diagnostics are:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `F1/F2` | `[2,2]` | `-1` | `30.5327796482886...` | 2 |

In every case the two-by-two height-pairing matrix has all four entries equal.
Both displayed points are non-torsion, while their difference has order 2.
Consequently the box in the two displayed points collapses to one visible free
direction plus torsion.  This proves rank at least 1, but it does not prove
rank exactly 1.  `RankBounds` was deliberately time-boxed: even with GRH class
group bounds it remained class-group dominated on these large elliptic models.

The reproducible bounded run was

```text
magma -b coeff_bound:=12 do_rank:=false do_mw:=false write_log:=true \
  log_file:=results/target_2248_hlp_pair_fibers.log \
  code/target_2248_hlp_pair_fibers.m
```

For **each** fiber it tested 196 elliptic-curve combinations, producing 25
distinct `q` values.  Exactly one value survived `C=c^2`; it was the original
HLP value

```text
q = 307598400/352612321.
```

The lift has exact torsion `[2,2,4,8]` of order 128, as expected, but is the
known split HLP example and gives no root-power simplicity certificate.  The
three reported lifts are the same curve, counted once from each pair fiber.
Full exact output is in

```text
results/target_2248_hlp_pair_fibers.log.
```

## 2. Rational Richelot graph of a simple `[2,4,8]` seed

The seed from `notes/m18_m14_halving.md` is

```text
y^2 =
  7061463847622250*x^5
  +104632219276049025*x^4
  +135735215960638800*x^3
  +188573481843278400*x^2
  +51200550567936000*x.
```

Magma gives exact torsion `[2,4,8]`.  At `p=47` its Frobenius polynomial is

```text
T^4 - 4*T^3 + 30*T^2 - 188*T + 2209,
```

and the minimal polynomials of `pi^n` have degree 4 for every `n=2,...,12`.
This is the strict geometric-simplicity certificate used throughout the
search.

The script

```text
code/target_2248_richelot_seed.m
```

breadth-first searches rational `RichelotIsogenousSurfaces`, normalizes every
Jacobian, deduplicates over `Q` using genus-2 invariants plus `IsIsomorphic`,
and computes exact torsion at every node.

Depth 1 has three rational neighbors:

```text
[2,2,2,4], [2,2,8], [2,2,8].
```

The first is the hoped-for full-2-torsion branch, but its 8-chain has already
fallen to a 4-chain.  Expanding through depth 2 gives 18 distinct Jacobians.
Two final nodes occur at depth 3; expanding both produces only old nodes.
Hence the rational component closes with 20 nodes and 54 examined directed
Richelot edges, no product surfaces, and no target.

Its exact torsion distribution is

```text
[2,2,2]    4
[2,2,2,4]  1
[2,2,4]    3
[2,2,8]    2
[2,4]      8
[2,4,8]    1
[4,4]      1
```

Every Jacobian in this component is geometrically simple because it is
isogenous to the certified simple source, and geometric simplicity is
invariant under isogeny.  The closing run was

```text
magma -b max_depth:=4 max_nodes:=100 write_log:=true \
  log_file:=results/target_2248_richelot_seed_depth4.log \
  code/target_2248_richelot_seed.m
```

The shorter depth-2 log is retained as
`results/target_2248_richelot_seed.log`.

## Recommended continuation

The fixed HLP fibers are useful diagnostics, but their only visible free
direction returns to the decomposable base point at the tested bounds.  The
fixed simple seed's rational Richelot graph is now completely known and also
misses the target.  The natural next search is therefore a **family-level
Richelot sieve**:

1. Enumerate the verified one-split `[2,4,8]` family from
   `code/m18_m14_one_split_search.m`, retaining only strict root-power-simple
   sources.
2. For each source, compute its rational Richelot neighbors before doing any
   expensive torsion calculation.
3. Prioritize neighbors with six rational Weierstrass points (full rational
   `J[2]`) and then compute exact torsion.
4. Stop on exact `[2,2,4,8]`; certify simplicity for free from the source
   isogeny, while recording the source and Richelot kernel as a compact proof.

The fixed seed shows that “gain full `J[2]`” and “retain an 8-chain” are
independent constraints.  Varying the one-split source is the smallest search
that can make those two conditions intersect without returning to the much
larger blind HLP cover search.
