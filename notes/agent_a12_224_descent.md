# Z/2 x Z/24 via exact 2-descent on the A(12) chart

Date: 2026-07-02.  New attack on the ideas.txt "should be easy" target
[2,24], replacing the cold per-point exact-halving searches
(search_A2_24_from_A12.m, cold through height 12) by the descent
machinery developed for [4,16] in M_1(8,4).

## Setup

Chart (a12_parameterization.tex), params (p,z,r):
curve Y^2 = f = R*F, F = Q^2 + R*ell^2 (deg 6, components: K_R quadratic,
K_F quartic with D_4 structure via F = (Q + ell sqrt(-R))(Q - ell sqrt(-R))),
order-12 class P12 = P4 + P6 with explicit Mumford u-polys
u4 = Q^monic, u6 = (R+x-1)^monic.

x-T descent (even degree): delta([u,v]) = u_monic(T) in A*/(A*^2 Q*);
P12 + T halvable for some rational 2-torsion T  <=>  delta(P12) in the
subgroup generated by delta(J(Q)[2]) -- squareclass membership, no more
per-point quartic solves.

## Main structural result

The component norm conditions (necessary for delta(P12) trivial),
computed as resultants and factored over Q(p,z,r)
(script: agent_a12_224_descent_setup.m):

```text
N1 = Norm_{K_R} delta(P12) == p*(p+r)   mod squares
N2 = Norm_{K_F} delta(P12) == p*(p+r)   mod squares
```

BOTH collapse to the SINGLE conic condition

```text
C: p*(p+r) = square,   freely parametrized by  r = (v^2 - p^2)/p.
```

So the C-level of the [2,24] halving problem costs NOTHING on the A(12)
chart: on the (p,z,v)-space the necessary norm conditions hold
identically, and the entire obstruction is concentrated in the second
stage (actual squareness in K_R and in the quartic K_F, modulo the
shared Q* scalar of the even-degree x-T map).

## Extra-2 locus and local diagnostic (2026-07-02, later)

Scripts: agent_a12_224_extra2_derive.m, agent_a12_224_local_diagnostic.m.

**Extra-2 structure.**  [2,24] needs 2-rank 2 (not the generic 2-rank 1
of f = R*F, factor type [2,4]).  The clean rank-2 route is F -> [2,2]
(f -> [2,2,2]).  BUT the resolvent cubic of F is IRREDUCIBLE over
Q(p,z,r), so the F-split locus is a genuine codim-1 condition, not a
free rational parametrization (contrast the residual-quartic split in
how_we_found_2220_examples.md).  Note R-split is free
(disc R = 4p^2+4pr+1 = square) but gives only [1,1,4] = 2-rank 1
(parity), so it does not help by itself.  Dimension count is still
favorable: rank-2 (1 condition) + halving (1 condition) on 3 params
= a 1-dim [2,24] family -- unlike [2,2,16] on A(8) which was
over-determined.

**Local diagnostic (the decisive check).**  Counting chart points over
F_q with P12 halvable (H), 2-rank >= 2 (R), and both (HR):

```text
q=11: chart 316   H=116  R=128  HR=32    all 32 have J(F_q) >= [2,24]
q=13: chart 544   H=168  R=188  HR=32    all 32 ...
q=17: chart 1728  H=656  R=784  HR=256   all 256 ...
q=19: chart 3016  H=1188 R=1476 HR=544   all 544 ...
q=23: chart 6260  H=2528 R=3008 HR=1116  all 1116 ...
```

The HR locus is nonempty (~18% of chart points) at every tested prime,
and every HR point carries the [2,24] structure over F_q.  So there is
NO local obstruction: [2,24] via A(12) is height-limited, not
obstructed (opposite of [4,16] over F_7).  Pushing height WILL find it.

**Consequence for the search.**  The running height-16 funnel already
flags TARGET_2_24 on any halvable rank-2 hit, so no change is required
to catch the target.  The rank-2 locus is thin over Q (resolvent cubic),
so a focused search would want to parametrize it; the practical
alternatives are (a) push funnel height (h20, h24 -- cheap, no
obstruction to fear), or (b) parametrize the rank-2 locus via
R-split (free) + one rational root of F (the [1,1,1,3] route, 2-rank 2),
which is more tractable than the F->[2,2] resolvent.

Cross-check: [2,26] and [2,28] are already known (ideas.txt) -- likely
from the Platonov-Petrunin fundamental-unit/Pell method, a different
construction.  If A(12) stays cold at large height, that method is the
natural alternative route to [2,24].

## Search results and the A(12) rank-2 bottleneck (2026-07-02, final)

Runs completed:

```text
brute funnel h12: 6.06M pts, 0 halvable
brute funnel h16: 3.42M pts, 0 halvable
R-split rank-2 h22 (z<=14): 1.15M R-split pts, F-root=0, 0 halvable
```

**The bottleneck is the rank-2 locus, and it is arithmetically thin on
A(12).**  Two independent obstructions to an efficient rank-2 search:

1. Full rank-2 locus F -> [2,2]: the split resolvent
   RES(u) = u^3 + 2P u^2 + (P^2-4R) u - Q^2 has numerator of degree 16 in
   r (64 in p, 64 in z, 15923 terms) and is irreducible -- NOT solvable
   for a chart parameter, so no clean 2-parameter rational chart of the
   rank-2 locus.
2. Tractable sub-locus R-split + F-root: R splits for free
   (r=(w^2-4p^2-1)/(4p)) but that only gives 2-rank 1 ([1,1,4]); adding a
   rational root of F reaches 2-rank 2 ([1,1,1,3]) but is only a 1-dim
   sub-locus of the 2-dim rank-2 locus, and its rational points are
   height-starved (F-root rate 5.6% at height 4, 0% by height 14-22).

So: NO local obstruction (mod-p diagnostic positive at every prime), but
the rational rank-2 points on A(12) sit at high height.  Order-24
halving itself is also height-limited (0 rational halvable through the
searched boxes despite ~37% halvable mod p).

## Verdict and recommended pivot

A(12) is not an efficient route to [2,24] at accessible height, despite
being obstruction-free.  Options, in priority order:
1. **Platonov-Petrunin fundamental-unit / Pell method** -- the likely
   source of the known [2,26] and [2,28]; directly targets [2, even]

   `fundamental_unit_rank_test.m`, `search_pell_unit_targets.m` (which

   next build.
2. A large many-core overnight brute-funnel run at height 20-24 (~50-100M
   pts) -- legitimate since obstruction-free, but slow and only weakly
   targeted (mostly finds [24], not [2,24]).
3. A different order-24 chart with the extra 2-torsion built in from the
   start (an "A(2,24)" analog), if one can be derived.

## Backward construction blueprint (the real next build)

Unit of degree n=24: alpha^2 - f*beta^2 = c (const), deg alpha = 24,
deg beta = 21, f = (alpha^2 - c)/beta^2 (deg 6, monic).  So:
  1. choose beta (deg 21) with structure making a constant c a square
     mod beta^2 (c a square in each Q[x]/beta_i for the irreducible
     factors beta_i, Hensel-liftable);
  2. solve alpha^2 == c mod beta^2 with deg(alpha) EXACTLY 24 (the hard
     part: a generic square root has degree 41; need beta engineered so
     a CRT-sign combination collapses the degree to 24);
  3. set f = (alpha^2-c)/beta^2; require monic, squarefree, 2-rank 2
     (factor type [2,2,2]/[1,1,2,2]) and D_infty order EXACTLY 24 (not a
     proper divisor -- exclude via CFOrder).
The degree-collapse in step 2 is the crux; the palindromic quasi-period
structure of the CF (period ends in 2*a_0) is the tool to force it.
This is a genuine research build (a session on its own).

## Backward CF construction: attempted, characterized, not completed (2026-07-03)

Attacked the construction from its tractable entry points:

1. **Symbolic forward CF** on a parametrized f: intractable (the Q_i
   polynomials blow up past ~6-8 of the needed ~24 steps).
2. **alpha^2 - f beta^2 = c** (deg alpha=24, deg beta=21, f=(alpha^2-c)/beta^2):
   writing c=gamma^2 and splitting beta=beta1*beta2 gives
      2*gamma = beta2^2*g2 - beta1^2*g1,   f = g1*g2,
   with deg g1+deg g2 = 6, deg beta1=(24-deg g1)/2, deg beta2=(24-deg g2)/2.
   For cyclic [24]: g1 deg 2, g2 deg 4 (beta1 deg 11, beta2 deg 10).  But
   this condition (beta2^2 g2 and beta1^2 g1 agree except in the constant)
   IS the CF-convergent/Pell condition -- circular.  The crux is
   engineering beta so a square root of c mod beta^2 has degree EXACTLY
   24 (generic degree 41); no clean handle found.
3. **Family scans with the exact CF-order filter** -- definitive negative:
   ```
   trinomial x^6+Cx^2+Dx+E,  |coeff|<=9:  MAX D_infty order = 6
   (x^2-1)(x^2+ax+b)(x^2+cx+d), grid H<=8:  no order >= 10
   ```
   f near a perfect square (small params) has small regulator; large
   order needs f far from square in a precise way -> construction-only.

**Status: not completed.**  The backward construction is a genuine
research build (the degree-collapse in step 2, or the palindromic-period
inversion in step 1).  Recommended alternatives:
 - a literature [2,2n] family (Leprevost/Kulesz have explicit genus-2
   torsion families; the source of the known [2,26],[2,28]);
 - dedicated work on the beta-engineering / period inversion.
Validated tooling (exact CF order, Pell Hankel filter) is in place for
whoever takes it up.
