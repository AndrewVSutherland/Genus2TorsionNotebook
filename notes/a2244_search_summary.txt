A(2,2,4,4) / M(2,2,4,8) Search Summary
================================================

Workspace: torsion_jac
Summary file: notes/a2244_search_summary.txt
Main running notes: notes/a2244_local_obstructions.md

Goal
----
We investigated the route from the K3 surface model for M(2,2,2,8) / M(2,2,4,8)
toward examples whose Jacobians have larger torsion, especially through a possible
A(2,2,4,4) stage and then an extra halving to M(2,2,4,8).

The central question became: are the repeated failures caused by blind height-search
limitations, or by local obstructions on the relevant K3 boundary strata?

Main Code Added or Changed
--------------------------
1. code/enumerate_surface_tuples.cpp
   - C++ enumerator for primitive positive sorted curve tuples on the K3 surface.
   - Added boundary component filtering: zero components and pair-collision strata.
   - Added grouped boundary specs:
       D = {Ead,Ebd,Ecd}
       N = {Za,Zb,Zc,Zd,Eab,Eac,Ebc}
       any = all boundary components
   - Added optional REAL_LOCAL_DEPTH filter. For sorted positive tuples, this checks
     the real-compatible partition 12|34 locally at the required primes to a chosen
     p-adic depth.
   - Added an early mod-p common A(2,2,4,4) partition prefilter. This shrinks the
     residue table before the expensive integer enumeration starts.

2. code/m2248_sieve.m
   - Existing exact Magma sieve was used repeatedly.
   - It checks A(2,2,4,4) square conditions across all permutations, then tests the
     intermediate and full M(2,2,4,8) halving equations.

3. code/analyze_a2244_boundary_strata.py
   - Diagnostic for exact signed Q_p squareclasses of A(2,2,4,4) partitions on
     generated tuple files.
   - Important correction: the p-adic unit sign must be retained. Using abs(n) is
     wrong at p=11 and p=23 because -1 is nonsquare.

4. code/analyze_a2244_padic_residues.py
   - Finite p-adic residue-class analyzer on the K3 surface.
   - Refines mod-p A(2,2,4,4)-ambiguous boundary classes to p^2.

5. code/analyze_a2244_padic_deep_charts.py
   - Samples p^3 behavior of normalized p^2-deep boundary charts.
   - This is a diagnostic chart sampler, not a full p^3 enumeration of every p^2 class.

6. code/a2244_padic_signature_sieve.py
   - Candidate-level signed finite-depth local sieve.
   - Computes oriented A(2,2,4,4) cross-difference squareclasses at 11^k and 23^k.
   - Tracks common partitions, real-compatible partitions, and exact signed Q_p status.

7. code/run_surface_tuple_chunks.py
   - Resumable chunk driver for long height searches.
   - Runs the C++ enumerator on ax-ranges, writes chunks atomically, and merges completed chunks.

Good-Reduction Obstruction
--------------------------
Finite-field checks at p=11 and p=23 showed that no good-reduction K3 point gives an
A(2,2,4,4) lift. Therefore any rational candidate must have bad reduction at both
11 and 23:

    p | abcd * product_{i<j}(a_i^2 - a_j^2)

This was the first major pruning principle. It moved the search away from blind height
enumeration and toward boundary strata at 11 and 23.

Boundary Strata Do Not Alone Force Failure
------------------------------------------
A simple boundary label such as Eij or Zi is too coarse to force A(2,2,4,4) failure.
For a fixed 2+2 partition, only the four cross-differences matter:

    a_i^2 - a_k^2, a_i^2 - a_l^2, a_j^2 - a_k^2, a_j^2 - a_l^2

They must have the same Q_p^*/Q_p^{*2} squareclass. A collision inside one side of the
partition may be harmless, while a cross-collision can force an odd valuation or a deep
ambiguity.

Conclusion:
  Boundary component labels alone do not prove impossibility. The obstruction is finer:
  partition compatibility, squareclass signs, p-adic depth, real signs, and finally the
  full M(2,2,4,8) equations.

Integrated Higher-Height Filtering
----------------------------------
The C++ enumerator now supports:

    REAL_LOCAL_DEPTH

When this is positive, the enumerator applies the real-compatible local depth filter to
positive sorted tuples. For sorted positive tuples, the real-compatible partition is 12|34.

A chunked driver was added:

    code/run_surface_tuple_chunks.py

This is needed because higher-height searches should be resumable and split by ax-range.

Important Caution About Signed Surface Representatives
------------------------------------------------------
The generated tuple files contain positive sorted curve tuples. They are obtained after
sign choices, clearing denominators, taking absolute values, sorting, and primitive
normalization. Therefore the positive tuple need not itself satisfy the symmetric K3
equation with all positive signs.

Consequence:
  K3 next-lift flags are diagnostic only for positive sorted tuple files. They should not
  be used as killers unless a signed surface representative is retained.

Current Overall Conclusions
---------------------------
1. Good reduction is impossible at p=11 and p=23 for A(2,2,4,4) points coming from this
   K3 surface. Candidates must lie on bad-reduction boundary strata at both primes.

2. The boundary strata alone do not force failure. Coarse boundary labels are too weak.
   The obstruction uses oriented squareclasses, partition compatibility, p-adic depth,
   real signs, and the full M(2,2,4,8) conditions.

3. Correct signed p-adic squareclasses are essential. Ignoring the sign of the unit gives
   wrong local conclusions at 11 and 23.

4. At height 10000, the tractable forced-boundary search gives 118 tuples. None produce
   an intermediate or full M(2,2,4,8) witness.

5. At height 10000, signed finite-depth filtering gives:
      p^3: 14 finite-local candidates, 4 real-compatible
      p^4: 1 finite-local candidate, 0 real-compatible
   Hence depth 4 plus real sign kills all height-10000 candidates.

6. The unique exact signed finite-local survivor is [605,845,2925,6877], with partition
   13|24. It is not real-compatible and is also bad at many auxiliary primes.

7. The most useful computational path forward is not blind height extension. It is a
   chunked higher-height search using:
      - forced bad reduction at 11 and 23,
      - boundary strata such as 11:N,23:N,
      - early common mod-p A(2,2,4,4) partition prefilter,
      - final signed depth-4 real-compatible 12|34 filter,
      - exact all-permutation M(2,2,4,8) sieve only on survivors.

8. The current B=20000 pilot with the improved filter produced no candidates for ax=1..10
   and ran in 35.660 seconds, versus 115.957 seconds before the early partition prefilter.
