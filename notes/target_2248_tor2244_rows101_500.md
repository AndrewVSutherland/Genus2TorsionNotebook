## Result

The corrected sweep completed all 400 requested rows.  It found two distinct
`Q`-isomorphism classes with exact source torsion `[2,4,8]`, both arising from
row 136 and the tuple

```text
[53312,54600,60088,61880].
```

They are reverse Richelot edges 13 and 14.  Each has a rational Richelot edge
1 whose codomain is a Cartesian product of elliptic curves (`SetCart`).  Thus
both sources are **proven geometrically nonsimple**, independently of the fact
that the strict root-power routine found no certificate on their displayed
models.  There were no certified-simple or unresolved `[2,4,8]` sources.

Every rational Richelot neighbor of both exact sources was enumerated.  For
each source there were three edges: edge 1 was the product quotient, edge 2 was
one-split, and edge 3 was full-Weierstrass.  Both full-Weierstrass occurrences
were tested exactly and had torsion `[2,2,4,4]` of order 64; they represent the
same `Q`-isomorphism class.  Hence this chunk produced no `[2,2,4,8]` target.
