# Arithmetic feasibility of the contact-30 genus-6 trigonal quotient

## Verdict

The genus-6 quotient is a legitimate classical-Chabauty target **only if**
one can first prove a Mordell--Weil rank bound below 6 and construct a
finite-index subgroup of its Jacobian.  The present computation does not do
that.  It finds three visible divisor directions and strong evidence that
they are independent, but no rank upper bound and no saturation theorem.

More importantly, the Jacobian is `Q`-simple.  Thus there is no elliptic,
genus-2, or other lower-genus quotient over `Q` on which to replace the
missing rank computation.  This makes the remaining step a genuine
general-nonhyperelliptic-Jacobian descent problem, not a routine Magma
Chabauty calculation.

The reproducible scripts are

```text
code/contact30_c3root_genus6_canonical.m
code/contact30_c3root_genus6_local_jacobian.m
code/contact30_c3root_genus6_discriminant_resolvent.m
```

## Canonical model

Let `D` denote the normalized quotient, with trigonal coordinates `(z,rho)`.
The cleared affine equation has bidegree `(10,3)`.  Magma computes its
canonical model as a degree-10 curve in `P^5`, cut out by

```text
six quadrics and three cubics.
```

The six quadrics cut out a nonsingular degree-4 rational normal scroll.  The
Clifford-index-one routine recovers the same degree-three map to `P^1_z`.
There is no second pencil or hidden canonical decomposition in this model.

## The six rational boundary places

Label the normalized rational places as follows:

| place | `z` | ramification in `z:D -> P1` | plane `rho` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `A` | `2` | 1 | 1 |
| `B` | `2` | 2 | 1 |
| `C` | `5` | 1 | -1 |
| `D0` | `5` | 2 | 0 |
| `E` | `14/3` | 3 | 1 |
| `F` | `32/7` | 1 | 1 |

The fiber over `32/7` also contains one closed point of degree 2.  The unique
place over `z=infinity` has degree 3, so it contributes no rational point.

The trigonal fibers give the exact divisor identities

```text
A + 2 B = C + 2 D0 = 3 E.
```

Consequently all differences among the six known rational points lie in the
subgroup generated by only three classes:

```text
B-E,  D0-E,  F-E.
```

This is an upper bound on the rank of the *visible subgroup*, not on
`rank J_D(Q)`.

## Exact reduction data at p=13

The reduction is good and has

```text
#J_D(F_13) = 5716043,
J_D(F_13)  = Z/5716043 Z,
```

where `5716043` is prime.  With `A` as base point, the five difference
classes have coordinates

```text
B-A   153257
C-A  3660056
D0-A 4039272
E-A  2007519
F-A  4855158                 (mod 5716043).
```

Every one has full order `5716043` after reduction.  The three visible
directions relative to `E` are

```text
B-E   3861781
D0-E  2031753
F-E   2847639                 (mod 5716043).
```

The exact relation lattice modulo 13 has minimum squared Euclidean norm
`8017`; its shortest vectors are

```text
+/-(78,-13,42).
```

Therefore there is no integral relation among the three visible classes of
squared coefficient norm below `8017`.  This is useful evidence for visible
rank 3, but it cannot prove independence: a global relation could be a
larger vector in the same reduction lattice.  A direct principal-divisor
test of the shortest vector was not retained because Magma's general
function-field test did not finish promptly.

The Frobenius polynomial is

```text
X^12 + 2 X^11 + 2 X^10 + 15 X^9 + 302 X^8 + 341 X^7
 + 437 X^6 + 4433 X^5 + 51038 X^4 + 32955 X^3
 + 57122 X^2 + 742586 X + 4826809.
```

It is irreducible over `Q`; its Galois group has order `46080`, the full
degree-6 Weyl group.  In particular `J_D` is `Q`-simple.  Any nonconstant
map from `D` to a curve of genus 1 through 5 would induce a proper nonzero
quotient of `J_D`, so no such map exists over `Q`.

## Discriminant resolvent and S3 closure

The squarefree discriminant resolvent is the genus-6 hyperelliptic curve

```text
H: y^2 = (z-2)(z-5)(31z^2-220z+352) P10(z),
```

where `P10` is the degree-10 branch polynomial printed in the script.  Its
branch degrees are `[1,1,2,10]`.  Magma found

```text
0 <= rank J_H(Q) <= 2
```

in 77 seconds, and a height-10000 point search found only

```text
(2,0), (5,0), (4,+8192), (4,-8192).
```

This does not bound `rank J_D(Q)`.  The resolvent parametrizes fibers whose
cubic discriminant is a square; a rational root with an irreducible
quadratic cofactor normally has nonsquare discriminant and does not lift to
`H(Q)`.

The `S3` Galois closure `X` has genus 18 and the standard representation
decomposition

```text
J_X  ~  J_H x J_D^2.
```

Thus the tractable rank bound on `J_H` is independent of the missing rank of
`J_D`; the Galois closure does not turn the original point problem into
hyperelliptic Chabauty.

## Why Chabauty cannot yet be run

Classical Chabauty needs a proven bound

```text
rank J_D(Q) < 6
```

and enough of a finite-index Mordell--Weil subgroup to compute its annihilating
differentials.  The known points supply neither an upper bound nor a proof of
finite index.  A Mordell--Weil sieve has the same missing input.

A degree-3 descent is not presently a shortcut: the trigonal extension has
full `S3` monodromy rather than a rational cyclic deck group, and the simple
Jacobian supplies no rational 3-isogeny or lower-dimensional factor.  A
covering collection would amount to a research-level descent on the Picard
group of this explicit nonhyperelliptic curve.

Combined with the independent computation that the geometric endomorphism
ring is `Z`, quadratic Chabauty gives no relaxed rank inequality: the
Neron--Severi rank is one, so its numerical condition is again `rank < 6`.

## Recommended next step

If this route is pursued, the next step should be narrowly scoped:

1. implement or import Khuri--Makdisi/general plane-curve Jacobian arithmetic
   for the canonical model;
2. compute heights and saturation for `B-E`, `D0-E`, and `F-E`;
3. obtain a rigorous 2- or 3-Selmer rank upper bound for the full
   six-dimensional simple Jacobian;
4. only if the upper bound is at most 5, run general-curve Coleman integration
   at `p=13` followed by a Mordell--Weil sieve.

The stop rule should be immediate: if step 3 is unavailable, do not spend
time on Coleman integration.  The missing rank bound, rather than genus 6
itself, is the decisive obstruction.
