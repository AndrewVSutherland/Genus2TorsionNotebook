## 4b. UPDATE 2026-08-01: BLP 2009 retrieved — survey answers the question

The full paper (Bernard–Leprévost–Pohst, *Jacobians of genus-2 curves

construction: C_{a,b,c,d}: y^2 = R^2 - 4c^2 S^2 with R = x^3-x^2+ax+b,

t_0(a,b,c,d) = t_1(a,b,c,d) = 0 (from psi = yU - V with

**The real prize is the method**: f = (R-2cS)(R+2cS) — two monic cubics g,

monic sextic with contact representations f = h_i^2 + c_i x^{a_i}(x-1)^{b_i},
each giving a relation a_i u' + b_i v' = (m_i - 3) w in J among
u = [P_0 - oo], v = [P_1 - oo], w = [oo+ - oo-] (branch decorations

representations (3x3 determinant); with only TWO (the family-with-room
hope, e.g. exponents (4,1) & (1,3) whose uv-minor is 11), the full
decorated minor-gcd analysis (all 16 sign choices) gives gcd = 1 in every
case -- the oo-column always breaks the 11.  Confirmed computationally:
the saturated (c1 c2 != 0) two-contact system is a clean 2-parameter
family (1-2 rational solutions per frozen (a,b); code/lep22_engine_setup.m,
results/lep22_engine_setup.log) and every sampled member has TRIVIAL
torsion.  This explains structurally why Leprevost's curves are isolated.
