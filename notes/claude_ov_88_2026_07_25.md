## 1. The route, and why it is the right one

Nicholls' Λ_334 is a 3-dimensional family of genus-2 curves carrying a marked
`(Z/4)^2 = Σ ⊆ J(Q)`. Parameters `(s,t,v)`; the member is built by
`MemberSTV(s,t,v)` (identical code in every script of this lane).

Verified chart facts (`results/claude_ov_88chart_verify.log`):

* identity `η² = s² + (1−t²)(t²−β²)`, `u = η/(sβ)` — **TEST1 175/175**;
* the two stage-1 x-T criteria agree with exact Magma torsion — **TEST2 235/235**.

Factor structure (`results/claude_ov_88lift_split.log`, 12825 members):
`bc` is a square identically (12825/12825), so `f1 = l1·l2·l3` has irreducible type
**[1,1,2,2]** (12556) or [1,1,1,1,2] (269). Type [1,1,2,2] ⟹ rational 2-torsion rank
exactly 2 ⟹ the 2-primary part of `J(Q)_tors` has **at most two cyclic factors**, hence

> `J(Q)_tors ⊇ Z/8 × Z/8` ⟺ `J(Q)[4] = Σ` and **all 12** order-4 elements of Σ are
> 2-divisible in `J(Q)`.

That reduces [8,8] to twelve independent halving questions, each decidable by the
even-degree x-T map. The necessary sublocus (**DS1**, "double stage 1") is cut out by
three square conditions `t²−1 = □`, `(t²−1)(s²−(t²−1)²) = □`, `t²−β² = □`; the whole
conic-base machinery exists to produce rational points *on that sublocus*.

### 2.4 **Deduplication — the sample was much smaller than it looked** (this session)

A member depends only on `(s², t², v)`: `A` and `a` depend on `s², t²`; `u` flips sign
with `t` and enters only through `u²`. Hence:

* the 108 (m,n) bases carry only **22 distinct `(s²,t²)`**;
* the 332 member records are only **68 distinct curves**;
* the 35 full-norm-kernel members of the box profile are only **7 distinct curves**

Any rate quoted per *record* on this family is inflated by a factor of ~4–5. All
numbers below are per **distinct curve**.

**A bug was found and fixed in the middle of this.** The first pass lost 14 of the 71
generator-bearing bases to `Runtime error in '!': Point does not lie in scheme`: the
2-torsion of the minimal model was being built as `Em![x,0]` from the roots of the

latent bug has been fixed in `code/claude_ov_88conic_base2.m`, where it never fired

selection: the crash could only occur *after* generators were found, so 14/71 = 20% of
the generator-bearing bases have a non-short minimal model — the loss was not random.

Yield curve measured on a fixed 398-base subset: bound 600 → 24 members (15 s);
bound 10⁴ → 44 members (24 s); bound 10⁶ → aborted at 2 min (cost blows up, yield

A **deeper pass over the same 8000 bases at search bound 10⁵** was then run and stopped

That pass also measures the coverage weakness directly: **on the 1790 bases processed

**Unconditional total: 564 distinct DS1 curves, 6768 order-4 classes, zero

### 2.7 Where exactly the lift dies (this session)

`code/claude_ov_88obs.gp`, records `data/claude_ov_88obs_recs.txt`, log
`results/claude_ov_88obs.log`.

On DS1 the component algebra is `L = Q × Q × K1 × K3`, `K1 = Q(√D1)`, `K3 = Q(√a)`.
Writing `u_D = λ_i·(square)` in each component, `D` is 2-divisible iff a **single**
rational `c` works in every component; since `Q* ∩ (K_i*)² = (Q*)² ∪ d_i(Q*)²`, the two
*rational* components force `c = λ_lin` exactly, and the test is

> `def1 := λ_lin·λ_1 ∈ {1, D1}` **and** `def3 := λ_lin·λ_3 ∈ {1, a}` modulo squares.

(That derivation is why the gate is *exact* here, not a heuristic; the earlier note's
phrase "two conic conditions" is loose — the condition is square-class equality, and
`{1,D1}` is strictly smaller than the group of norms from `K1`.)

Over the 185 distinct `(D1, a, def1, def3)` records of the 1020 classes:

* exact condition: **0 records pass**, at either component;
* the strictly weaker **everywhere-local** relaxation `def_i ∈ N(K_i*)` — i.e. the
  conic `z² − d_i w² = def_i y²` soluble over every `Q_p` and `R` — passes at
  component 1 for **1 record**, at component 3 for **2**, and at **both for 0**;
* the number of obstructing places at component 1 is `2 (22×), 4 (59×), 6 (54×),
  8 (31×), 10 (8×), 12 (3×), 14 (6×), 18 (1×)` — always even, as the product formula
  requires (another self-check);
* the obstructing places scatter: **171 distinct primes** at component 1, 89 at
  component 3, with **no universal one** — the most frequent are 7 (56/185), 3 (44),
  11 (38), the real place (37), 23 (36), 43 (32);
* but the obstruction is always *cheap to see*: the **smallest** obstructing place is
  ≤ 3613 on every record.

**Interpretation.** The [8,8] route on the DS1 locus does not fail by a subtle global
(Sha-like) mechanism, and it does not fail by one fixed congruence: for essentially
every fibre the λ-compatibility conic is already insoluble over several `Q_p`, at primes
that vary from fibre to fibre. Combined with §2.1 (no mod-p obstruction to [8,8]
itself), the picture is: the DS1 locus has plenty of `F_p`-points with `(Z/8)²`, but its
*rational* points sit at square classes that are locally incompatible with halving. On
this evidence the locus is **thin**, not obstructed — and the thinness bites at the
`def` layer.

## 4. What is GRH-conditional

Nothing in claims 1–4. Explicitly:

* The 4 h 10 m conic-base harvest ran with `SetClassGroupBounds("GRH")` because it
  called `MordellWeilGroup`. Every curve it produced has been **re-derived and
  re-tested without it** (§2.5). GRH would only ever have affected the *completeness*
  claim "these are all the members reachable from this base" — a coverage statement,
  not a per-curve one — and that claim is not made anywhere in this note.
* The 479 curves of §2.6 never touched `MordellWeilGroup` at all.

## 5. What I did **not** check — read this before quoting the negative

* **Coverage is the weak point, and it is now quantified.** The extension only sees
  base points whose `E_{s,t}` has a generator below the search bound; at bound 10⁴,
  7929 of 8000 bases (99.1%) contributed nothing. Raising the bound to 10⁵ on the first
  1804 bases **doubled** the generator-bearing count (57 → 117 on the 1790 common
  bases), so the sample is nowhere near saturated: the negative samples the *low-height*
  stratum of the DS1 locus, and a base point whose generators are tall is invisible to
  it. That is precisely the stratum the (killed) MW route was reaching.
* **300 of the 400 (m,n) bases of the killed sweep were never completed**, and their
  expensive tail is exactly the interesting one: children 4 and 5 had each spent >1.5 h
  inside a single `MordellWeilGroup` call.
* The mod-p test (§2.1) enumerates the **affine** chart only; the boundary at infinity
  of `(s,t,β)`-space is not covered, at any prime.
* The DS1 conditions are *necessary* for [8,8] by the argument in
  `claude_ov_88modp.m`'s header, which rests on the verified chart identity — I did not
  independently re-derive that necessity this session.
* No 2-descent, no Selmer/Sha computation. "Local" in §2.7 means the λ-compatibility
  conic, **not** a Selmer group: I have not shown the halving torsor is
  everywhere-locally soluble-or-not, only its `def` shadow.
* The gate's two known failure shapes ([2,2,2] with no odd-degree factor; [2,4]) are
  *observed* not to occur on Λ_334 (564/564 are [1,1,2,2]); I did not prove they cannot.
* **No geometric-simplicity certificate was computed for any member** — correctly, since
  every member came back `[4,4]` and there is nothing to certify. If a hit ever appears,
  the full two-prime certificate is still owed.
* Λ_334 is **one** family. Nothing here bounds [8,8] on genus-2 Jacobians in general,
  and nothing here says the DS1 locus has no rational point with `[8,8]` — only that
  564 of them do not.
* The 27 members of shape [1,1,1,1,2] (torsion `[2,4,4]`, 2-rank 3) were logged but not
  pursued; they cannot give [8,8] (three cyclic 2-primary factors) but their `[2,4,4]`
  is itself worth a glance for other targets.
