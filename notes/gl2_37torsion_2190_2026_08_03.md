## Answer: YES, on some abelian surface isogenous to A_f — PROVEN.

AC's own data is a prediction: their code computes g = gcd-of-local-orders
and prints "predicted ... equal to bound" when it matches their Eisenstein
heuristic; their comment states g "divides the torsion order of an abelian
variety isogenous to A_f" with equality only under a unique-prime-above
criterion that FAILS here (37 splits in Q(sqrt3), 31 splits in Q(sqrt2)).
The paper's Sec. 4 lists are conjectural.  We proved the existence half:

**Theorem (computed, 2026-08-03).**  For f = 2190.2.a.v (Hecke field
Q(sqrt3), AL signs (-,-,-,+)): a_ell(f) == 1 + ell (mod a prime above 37)
for ALL primes ell.  Hence rhobar_{f,p37} is reducible with
semisimplification 1 + chibar_cyc, and by the standard isogeny-shift
argument some abelian surface B isogenous to A_f over Q has a rational
point of order 37.  Same for 1830.2.a.q with 31.

Proof method (code/gl2_eisenstein_congruence.m, results/gl2_eis_*.log):
on the f-isotypic piece of the sign-0 modular symbols of level N with its
Hecke-stable integral lattice L, the intersection of
ker(T_ell - (1+ell)) over all ell <= Sturm(N) = 888 (resp. 732) together
with ker(T_p - a_p^Eis) at p | N (Eisenstein targets (1,1,1,73) resp.
(1,1,1,61); note 73 == -1 mod 37 and 61 == -1 mod 31 match f's w_p = +1)
is NONZERO on L/PL: dimension 2 in both cases (one per sign eigenspace).
Since T is generated by Hecke operators up to the Sturm bound, the
eigensystem of f reduces to the Eisenstein system mod a prime above P
identically; Brauer-Nesbitt gives the reducible semisimplification; a
Galois-stable line is 1 (rational 37-point on A_f itself) or chibar
(quotient by the corresponding rational subgroup scheme C gives B = A_f/C
with a Galois-fixed line in the image of A_f[p]/C, i.e. a rational
37-point on B).
Validation: the identical code at level 23, P = 11 reproduces the
classical Eisenstein kernel (dim 2) of J_0(23).

## The point is NOT cuspidal (proven)

Since N is squarefree, all 16 (resp. 16) cusps of X_0(N) are rational and
the cuspidal subgroup is entirely rational.  Direct computation via the
rational period mapping (code/gl2_cusp_image_order.m; validated at level

difference (c) - (oo) in the optimal quotient A_f has order 1, for both
2190.2.a.v and 1830.2.a.q.  Hence the entire cuspidal subgroup dies in
these quotients and the 37/31-torsion is non-cuspidal — these are
"invisible-Eisenstein" cases (contrast X_0(23), where the same machinery
IS cuspidal).  Consistency: Magma's subvariety-side TorsionMultiple for
2190.2.a.v is exactly 37 (torsion is trivial or Z/37 there), lower bound
1 by cuspidal methods.
