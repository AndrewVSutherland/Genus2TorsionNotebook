## Conclusion

The independent-`3` problem above the rational endpoint-`R3` family is now
an explicit connected degree-`24` cover, rather than an undifferentiated
degree-`40` contact calculation.

The sign quotient `M=L^2` has one universal repeated-support point and two
contact-open pieces of degrees

```text
12 + 27.
```

The degree-`12` piece is the orthogonal support cover and is the only piece
that can contain a second rational `3`-torsion class.  It is irreducible over
`Q(e)`.  Its recovered `M` is not a square in its function field, so adjoining
`L` gives a connected degree-`24` actual-point cover.  In fact `L` itself is
primitive: its exact norm polynomial has degree `24` and is irreducible.

After the exact base change `e=e(u)` to `P8`, good reduction at `7` remains
irreducible of degree `24`.  Thus the relevant target for rational points is
genuinely degree `24`; the degree-`12` quotient parametrizes supports
`{Q,-Q}`, not rational signed classes `Q`.

No rational point is asserted or found here.  The global `P8` route remains
open, but it has been reduced to one explicit cover with a strong modular
localization.


## The exact degree-12 support polynomial

Put `a=1/e` and retain the contact coordinate `v` from

```text
q3(x) = x^2 + U*x + v^2.
```

The monic polynomial of the orthogonal support component is

```text
Phi12(v) =
    v^12
  + (4*a/3)*v^11
  + (6 + 10*a/3)*v^10
  + (10 + 4*a/3 - 2*a^2/3)*v^9
  - (15 + 10*a + 7*a^2/3 + 2*a^3/9)*v^8
  - (18 + 16*a + 6*a^2 + 8*a^3/9)*v^7
  - (14*a + 28*a^2/3 + 14*a^3/9)*v^6
  - (18 + 22*a + 26*a^2/3 + 10*a^3/9)*v^5
  - (15 + 12*a + 3*a^2 + 2*a^3/9)*v^4
  + (10 + 10*a + 10*a^2/3 + 10*a^3/27)*v^3
  + (6 + 22*a/3 + 10*a^2/3 + 2*a^3/3 + 4*a^4/81)*v^2
  + (4*a/3 + 4*a^2/3 + 4*a^3/9 + 4*a^4/81)*v
  + (1 + a/3)^4.
```

Equivalently, its coefficients over `Q(e)` have numerator and denominator
degrees at most `4`.  Exact factorization proves that `Phi12` is irreducible.

The full support count has the symplectic explanation

```text
40 = 1 + 12 + 27.
```

The marked order-`3` direction is the degree-`1` repeated-support section

```text
(M,U,v) = (1,-2,1),    U^2-4*v^2 = 0.
```

For a rational second class `Q`, the Weil pairing with the marked class `P`
must lie in `mu_3(Q)={1}`.  Hence `Q` lies in `P^perp`, giving `12` supports
modulo sign.  The other `27` supports are nonorthogonal and cannot produce a
rational `Q`.


## Recovery maps and contact-open certificate

Over `Q(e)[v]/(Phi12)`, the exact computation recovers polynomials

```text
M = m(v),    U = u(v),
```

both of degree `11`.  Their explicit coefficients are generated by the
script; `m(v)` has coefficient numerator/denominator degrees at most `8/5`,
and `u(v)` at most `9/6`.  The remaining coordinates are

```text
N = (3*U+6*M)/2,
R = (3*U^2+3*v^2+(2/e-15)*M-N^2)/2.
```

Rather than relying on interpolation, the script substitutes these maps into
all three residual contact equations and reduces them modulo `Phi12`; every
remainder is identically zero over `Q(e)`.  It also checks

```text
gcd(Phi12,M) = gcd(Phi12,v) = gcd(Phi12,U^2-4*v^2) = 1.
```

Thus this is the contact-open degree-`12` component, not a boundary factor.
Separately, multiplication by `U^2-4*v^2` in the exact length-`40` quotient
has stable rank `39`, and its kernel is exactly the universal length-`1`
section above.


## The signed degree-24 cover

Let

```text
F12 = Q(e)[v]/(Phi12).
```

The exact square test gives

```text
M notin F12^2.
```

Consequently

```text
F24 = F12(L),    L^2=M
```

has degree `24` over `Q(e)`.  A one-variable primitive polynomial is

```text
Phi24(L) = Res_v(Phi12(v), L^2-m(v)).
```

It is monic, even in `L`, has degree `24`, and has coefficient
numerator/denominator degrees at most `10` in `e`.  Exact factorization proves
it irreducible.  Over `Q(e)[L]/(Phi24)`, the gcd

```text
gcd_v(Phi12(v), L^2-m(v))
```

is linear, so it recovers `v` rationally from `L`; the displayed maps then
recover `U,N,R`.  Thus `L`, not merely a generic linear combination, is a
primitive element of the actual-point cover.


Their exact degrees and coefficient shapes are


This is a good-reduction irreducibility certificate for the characteristic-
zero P8 pullback.  It also guards against the tempting but incorrect reading
of the degree-`12` quotient as a family of rational signed points.
