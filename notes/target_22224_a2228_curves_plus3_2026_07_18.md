# Explicit full-cover curves plus rational 3-torsion

Date: 2026-07-18

## Outcome

No geometrically simple `[2,2,2,24]` example was found on the four known
explicit rational curves on the full `A(2,2,2,8)` cover, through rational
parameter height `100000`.

The exhaustive denominator-aware CRT search used 44 independently computed
projective point-count masks, at every prime from the search list through
`199`.  The only first-stage output was 140 parameters on Adam's curve.  Every
one of those 140 is killed by a good-reduction prime in the independent range
`211..443`; none reached `TorsionSubgroup`.

The newly noticed product-surface fibers `(1,r,s,rs)` were also analyzed.  They
are bielliptic and therefore cannot give a geometrically simple record.
Nevertheless, their rational 3-torsion covers have useful low-genus geometry
and may be valuable as seeds away from the product surface.

## Critical correction to the cubic-contact equations

The older `Aaux` implementation is incorrect: it doubles the
`-(M+3U)^2` contribution.  All contact masks made with that formula must be
discarded unless independently revalidated.

For

```text
f = x^5 + e1*x^4 + e2*x^3 + e3*x^2 + e4*x,
q = x^2 + U*x + v^2,
M = L^2,
```

the correct eliminated equations are

```text
P  = 4*M*e1 + 12*(U^2+v^2) - (M+3*U)^2,
G1 = (M+3*U)*P + 16*v^3 - 8*M*e2 - 8*U^3 - 48*U*v^2,
G2 = P^2 + 64*(M+3*U)*v^3 - 64*M*e3
     - 192*(U^2*v^2+v^4),
G3 = P*v^3 - 4*M*e4 - 12*U*v^4.
```

With

```text
h = x^3/L + (M+3U)*x^2/(2L) + P*x/(8L) + v^3/L,
```

these equations give the exact identity

```text
h^2 - f = q^3/M.
```

The corrected implementation was checked point-for-point against
`3 | #J(F_p)` and then by constructing `D=[q,h mod q]` and asserting `3D=0`.
The aggregate validation counts were:

```text

p=17: checked 47 smooth specializations, 19 positive by both methods

```

## Product-surface fibers

For

```text
(a,b,c,d) = (1,r,s,r*s),
k = r*s,
```

the genus-two curve has the rational involution

```text
iota(x,y) = (k^2/x, k^3*y/x^3).
```

Indeed, `f(k^2/x)=k^6*f(x)/x^6`.  Hence its Jacobian is `(2,2)`-isogenous
over `Q` to two elliptic quotients and is not geometrically simple.  With
`u=x+k^2/x`, the quotients are

```text
E_+: v^2 = (u+2k)(u+1+k^2)(u+r^2+s^2),
E_-: v^2 = (u-2k)(u+1+k^2)(u+r^2+s^2).
```

The fixed auxiliary fibers are

```text
t=1/2: s=(9-16r)/(16-9r),
t=2/3: s=(25-144r)/(144-25r).
```

Since the isogeny degree is prime to 3, rational 3-torsion on the Jacobian is
equivalent to rational 3-torsion on one of these elliptic quotients.  Pulling
back the elliptic 3-division quartic gives, for both fixed fibers:

| quotient | genus of rational x-coordinate cover | genus after adjoining y |
|---|---:|---:|
| `E_+` | 3 | 5 |
| `E_-` | 0 | 0 |

The bivariate 3-division polynomials are irreducible of bidegree `(16,4)`.
Eliminating the `x`-coordinate on the minus quotient gives an irreducible plane
cover of bidegree `(48,8)`, also with function-field genus zero.  The explicit
calculations are in:

```text
code/target_22224_product_surface_elliptic_quotients.m
code/target_22224_product_surface_plane_cover.m
results/target_22224_product_surface_elliptic_quotients.txt
```

This genus-zero cover is the natural place to continue if the product surface
is used as a boundary seed, but any final record candidate must leave the
bielliptic locus.
