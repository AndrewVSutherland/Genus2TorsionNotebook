Goal (Filip): upgrade as many (infty) entries of Table 1 (tab:census, the
geometrically simple census) to infty as possible.  Per the paper's own
convention, an infty entry requires three legs:
  (L1) a positive-dimensional family with marked subgroup G over the
       function field, and generic exactness, which per the paper's
       convention follows from the marked subgroup plus ONE fiber with
       exact torsion G;
  (L2) generic geometric simplicity, from ONE strict fiber (irreducible
       Frobenius charpoly at a good prime p with [Q(pi^n):Q] = 4 for all
       n <= 12);
  (L3) infinitely many rational fibers giving pairwise distinct curves:
       a rational base plus a nonconstant modulus map (witnessed by two
       fibers with distinct G2-invariants; a nonconstant coordinate of
       the G2 map takes each value finitely often on P^1(Q)).

## Round 2b (2026-08-15): [4], [2,4], [2,2,4] via automatic halving

The construction (fixes the refuted naive criterion): define
  q_{alpha,beta}(x) = x^2 + ((2*alpha*beta+1)/beta^2) x + alpha^2/beta^2.
Its root theta satisfies the IDENTITY  -theta = (alpha + beta*theta)^2
(direct check using beta^2 theta^2 = -(2ab+1)theta - a^2), and
q_{alpha,beta}(0) = (alpha/beta)^2 is a square.  Hence on any model
f = x * (product of such q's), every component of the x-T descent image
of e = [(0,0) - infty] is a square (the finite-factor components are
-theta, squares by the identity; the self-component is the product of
the q(0)'s, a square; cf. Stoll 2001), so e is divisible by 2 and its
half D has exact order 4.  Empirical confirmation: 23/23 tested fibers
across the three families below show exactly the predicted groups.

## Round 6 (2026-08-16): [2,10] via Elkies' 5-torsion chart + 3 Weierstrass points

Source: Elkies, "Families of genus-2 curves with 5-torsion", Contemp. Math.
796 (2024), Thm 2.3 / Cor 2.4 / Sec 2.3.2.  Chart (L,L') = (x,1),
Q = q2 x^2 + q1 x + q0; the curve y^2 = g,
  g = 4Q^3 + (x^2-6x+1)Q^2 + 2x(x-1)Q + x^2,
carries the typical 5-torsion class T = [D-K], D = {Q=0, y=0} (Thm 2.3).
A rational Weierstrass point at x = lam imposes Elkies (2.22); dividing by
(LL')^2 it becomes  lam^2 q^2 + 2 lam q(2q-1)(q-1) + (q-1)^2 = 0 with
q = Q(lam)/lam, whose lam-discriminant/4 is q^2(q-1)^2 * 4q(q-1): rational
iff q(q-1) is a square.  Conic parametrization: q = t^2/(t^2-1),
m = t/(t^2-1), lam = (q-1)(-(2q-1)+2m)/q.

FAMILY: three parameters t1,t2,t3 -> three (lam_i, q_i); Q = the unique
quadratic interpolating Q(lam_i) = q_i lam_i.  Then g(lam_i) = 0 by
construction: three rational Weierstrass roots give the rational 2-torsion
classes [{lam_i,lam_j}] = (Z/2)^2, and with T: marked Z/5 x (Z/2)^2 =
[2,10] over the parameter field.  Slice: (t1,t2,t3) = (t,2,3), t free.
