## Symbolic cover

Variables:

```text
r,p,t,beta,U,V,H0,H1,H2,H3,Kappa
```

The equations are:

```text
coeffs_0..6 of f - H^2 - Kappa*(x^2+U*x+V)^3
Wsplit(r,p,t,beta)=0
```

where `Wsplit` is the cleared numerator of

```text
beta^2*(2*d + a + beta^2)^2 - 4*(d^2+c)*beta^2 - b^2.
```

Equation sizes:

```text
contact_c0 degree 12 terms 86
contact_c1 degree  9 terms 51
contact_c2 degree  9 terms 41
contact_c3 degree  6 terms 18
contact_c4 degree  6 terms 20
contact_c5 degree  3 terms  6
contact_c6 degree  3 terms  4
wsplit    degree  9 terms 28
disc(q3)  degree  2 terms  2
```

The W-split equation is irreducible over `Q` in this chart.  So there is no
obvious component split hiding at the top level; the useful cuts need to come
from contact invariants or projections.

## Triangular reduction on a fixed discriminant

There is a useful exact reduction on the chart `H3=M != 0`.  Fix

```text
disc(q3)=Delta,  so  V=(U^2-Delta)/4.
```

Then the high-degree contact coefficients solve successively for

```text
Kappa, H2, H1, H0
```

in terms of `(r,p,t,beta,U,M)`.  The remaining equations are the residual
coefficients `c2,c1,c0` plus W-split.

For `Delta=2` the residual sizes are:

```text
residual_c2 degree 17 terms  564
residual_c1 degree 21 terms 1381
residual_c0 degree 26 terms 2848
wsplit      degree  9 terms   28
```

For `Delta=3,5` the sizes are essentially identical.  So the fixed-Delta
elimination problem is large but not hopeless; the high-degree contact
variables should not be left in the Groebner basis.

## First exact elimination attempt

I tried the advertised move for `Delta=2`: eliminate `p` from the triangular
fixed-Delta equations using the W-split quadratic.  With no extra slice, the
three resultants have sizes:

```text
res(W,c2) degree 54 terms  50518
res(W,c1) degree 67 terms 142293
res(W,c0) degree 82 terms 343862
common gcd degree 2 terms 1
```

The common gcd is just the excluded `r=0` boundary.  The same pattern holds
for `Delta=3` and `Delta=5`.  So there is no easy hypersurface component in
the `(r,t,beta,U,M)` projection; any useful fixed-Delta family is a genuine
complete-intersection curve/surface piece, not a shared factor.

### Zero-slice tests

Exact Groebner on `Delta=2, U=0, beta=1` is boundary-dominated without
saturation (`dimension 2`).  Forcing `r*t*M != 0` by a Rabinowitsch variable
became too slow, so this is not the right first exact chart.

A finite-field zero-slice scan of the triangular equations for `Delta=2`
found populated small slices:

```text
ell=7:  74 solutions, 62 non-d0, top slices include U=0 beta=1,6 and U=6 beta=1,6
ell=11: 32 solutions, 16 non-d0, top slice U=0 beta=0
```

The recurring exact slice `Delta=2, U=0, beta=0` is special.  At `beta=0`,
the W-split equation collapses to

```text
W ~ (t-1)^2*(p-r)^2
```

after clearing the usual `r,t` factors.  The exact p-resultants on this slice
have

```text
common gcd = M^8*(t-1)^8*r^14.
```

Thus the only non-boundary projection factor is the explicit branch `t=1`;
the complementary branch is `p=r`.

### Beta-zero branches

I split `Delta=2, U=0, beta=0` into:

```text
branch A: t=1
branch B: p=r
```

After forcing the chart denominators nonzero, both branches have dimension 1.
The residual `c1` vanishes identically on both branches, leaving two equations
in three variables.

Eliminating `M` gives plane resultants:


After removing boundary factors, each is a square of a single large
irreducible plane factor:

```text

```

So the beta-zero slice does give honest curves, but not visibly rational
ones.

Bounded rational search on these two branch curves, using the residual linear
in `M^2`, found no rational points up to height `30`.

## Interpretation

This is a genuine positive signal for the open door.

The literal W-split/contact cover has many non-`d=0` contact points over
finite fields, and by `ell=13` it already has irreducible contact conics.
So the `d=0` rank-0 result is not reflecting a hidden local obstruction on
the W-split cover.

The exact discriminant fibers also behave the way a useful specialization
should: fixing `disc(q3)` does not kill the non-`d=0` irreducible branch.
At `ell=19`, for example, the fiber `disc(q3)=18` has 19 non-`d=0`
irreducible contacts.  That is curve-sized, not dust.

One subtlety: the earlier reconnaissance note used `wsplit` as shorthand for
all `2-rank >= 2` A(8) points.  This script is narrower: it is the literal
`W=Q^2+q` split cover parametrized by `beta`.  That is the right cover for
the current algebraic attack, but it explains why these counts are smaller
than the broader `2-rank >= 2` finite-field survey.

## Next exact move

Use the triangular fixed-Delta equations, not the full 11-variable system.
The first exact try shows that plain p-resultants are tractable but do not
split off an easy component.  Concretely, the next escalation should be:

1. Work on `disc(q3)=Delta` and `H3=M != 0`.
2. Use the triangular formulas to remove `Kappa,H2,H1,H0,V`.
3. Eliminate `p` first with W-split.
4. Use modular scans to choose exact `(U,beta)` or branch slices.
5. For each curve slice, eliminate `M`, then compute genus or run a
   Mordell-Weil/Chabauty-style point search.

The first Delta values worth trying are small representatives compatible with
the recurring finite-field fibers, especially `Delta=2,3,5,6,7,8` and also
the known cyclic-Z/24 squareclass `2` from curve A.

For `Delta=2`, the first exact branch worth deeper treatment is not
`U=0,beta=0`: it appears point-poor through height `30` and leads to large
degree-16/18 plane curves.  The next candidates should come from cross-prime
finite-field slice histograms, preferably avoiding `beta=0` unless the branch
factorization is unusually simple.

The first non-beta-zero candidates from that histogram,
`(Delta,U,beta)=(2,-2,2)` and `(3,0,2)`, are also point-poor through height
20, and the rational-grid escalation did not improve this.  The new
`(Delta,U,beta)=(3,0,3/2)` slice survives through `17` and then dies at `19`.

The next useful escalation is no longer simply to widen fixed
`(U,beta)` slices.  Better targets are:

```text
1. eliminate M on the strongest fixed slices and compute their actual curve
   genera, to turn the point-poor evidence into geometric evidence; or
2. stop freezing both U and beta, and instead analyze a one-parameter family
   such as Delta=3, U=0 with beta free, where the finite-field points may move
   rather than disappear modulo larger primes.
```

## Genus of the curve slices (2026-07-09)

Executing "Next exact move" #1 — eliminate M and compute the actual genus of
the slice curves, converting point-poverty into Faltings finiteness.
Code: `code/agent_a2_24_wsplit_slice_genus.m` (reuses `TriangularSystem`
verbatim; strips the shared `M^k` boundary factor before the M-resultant,
then `Genus` on each plane factor).

**beta=0 branches (the recurring Delta,U=0,beta=0 slice).**  With `beta=0`,
`wsplit` factors as `(t-1)^2 * r^3 * (r-p)^2`, giving the two branches.  After
`GCD(c2,c0)=M^k` is stripped and `M` eliminated, the main plane factor
(matching the note's earlier "degree-16 / degree-18 factor, squared") has:

```text
             main factor   genus (Delta=2)   genus (Delta=3)
 t=1 branch    degree 16        21               20
 p=r branch    degree 18        27               26
 (small side factors: t=1 deg-3 genus 1;  p=r deg-5 genus 4)
```

So the beta=0 branch curves have **genus ~20-27**.  By Faltings each carries
only FINITELY many rational points; the earlier height-30 empty search on
these branches is therefore essentially final, not merely a height limit.
This is the geometric evidence the "Next exact move" asked for: the beta=0
slice is closed.

Together with the `d=0` genuine cover being genus-1 rank-0
(`agent_a2_24_d0_derivation.md`), every curve slice of the W-split contact
cover analyzed so far is genus >= 1 with no low-height rational point — strong
evidence the 2-dimensional cover `X` is not unirational and that a rational
`[2,24]` point (if one exists) is sparse / high-height, consistent with the
"no local obstruction, globally thin" reading.

### The U=0, beta-free curve (recommendation #2): genus not tractable by elimination

Code: `code/agent_a2_24_wsplit_betafree_genus.m`.  On `Delta=3, U=0` with beta


but the next stage (eliminate `M`) of these degree-54..82 polynomials blows up

therefore high-degree; obtaining its genus needs either a lower-degree
birational model (a projection where the curve is small-degree) or a direct
Chabauty/descent on a chosen `disc(q3)=Delta` fiber, not naive resultants.
