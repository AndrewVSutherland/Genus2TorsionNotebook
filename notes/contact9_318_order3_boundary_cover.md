# Contact-9 order-3 cover above the live 7-adic boundary

## Scope

This resolves the first question left open by
`task4_boundary_component_audit.md`: the ordinary-node chart

```text
t = eps*s = 4 mod 7
```

really does carry smooth order-3 cover branches independent of the marked
order-9 class.  A bounded rational reconstruction on all such node-free
independent branches found no rational specialization.

The reproducible files are

```text
code/contact9_318_order3_boundary_lift.sage
code/contact9_318_order3_boundary_check.m
data/contact9_318_order3_boundary_h200_p16.txt
data/contact9_318_order3_boundary_magma_p7_t4.txt
```

## The correct order-3 identity

Write

```text
C: y^2=f(x),             deg(f)=5,
q=x^2+u*x+v,
H=h3*x^3+h2*x^2+h1*x+h0.
```

On the open locus where `h3 != 0`, `q` is squarefree, and
`gcd(q,f)=1`, the degree-two Mumford class

```text
D=(q,H mod q)
```

has order dividing 3 exactly when

```text
H^2-f=h3^2*q^3.                                      (1)
```

Indeed, `H-y` has a pole of order 6 at infinity.  At each root of `q`,
`H+y` is a unit after choosing the point `y=H`, so (1) gives

```text
div(H-y)=3(P1+P2)-6*infinity=3D.
```

Conversely, a function with divisor `3D` lies in
`L(6*infinity)=<1,x,x^2,x^3,y>`.  Away from the excluded loci it can be
scaled to `H-y`; taking its norm gives (1), and comparison of leading
coefficients gives the factor `h3^2`.  Thus this is the complete generic
degree-two order-3 cover, not just a sufficient ansatz.

If `f=sum(f_i*x^i)`, its six equations are

```text
2*h3*h2-f5-3*h3^2*u = 0
h2^2+2*h3*h1-f4-3*h3^2*(u^2+v) = 0
2*h3*h0+2*h2*h1-f3-h3^2*(u^3+6*u*v) = 0
h1^2+2*h2*h0-f2-3*h3^2*(u^2*v+v^2) = 0
2*h1*h0-f1-3*h3^2*u*v^2 = 0
h0^2-f0-h3^2*v^3 = 0.
```

These are six equations in six fiber variables over the contact-9
`t`-line.

## Special fiber and local normalization

At `t=4 mod 7`,

```text
f=(x+1)^2*(x^3+4*x^2+6*x+5).
```

Exhaustive enumeration gives 12 signed solutions, or six `q`-pairs.
Three pairs contain the node `x=6`; their fiber Jacobian has rank 5.
The three node-free pairs are

```text
q=x^2+x+4,   H=5*x^3+2*x^2+2*x+3, det=6
q=x^2+2*x+3, H=5*x^3+6*x^2+4*x+1, det=6
q=x^2+3*x+3, H=  x^3+5*x^2+5*x+2, det=5.
```

All determinants are nonzero in `F_7`.  Hence the order-3 cover itself
is already normal and etale over the `t`-line at these points: no blowup
of these cover branches is needed, even though the genus-2 special curve
is nodal.  Each point uniquely lifts over every `t in Z_7` with
`t=4 mod 7`.

Magma independently obtains the same zero-dimensional fiber, all 12
solutions, and the same ranks and determinants.

## Independence in the generalized Jacobian

The normalization of the nodal curve is

```text
E: Y^2=x^3+4*x^2+6*x+5,       y=(x+1)*Y.
```

It has `E(F_7)=[9]`.  The marked order-9 point becomes

```text
P=(1,3),  2P=(3,3),  3P=Qminus=(6,4),
Qplus=(6,3)=-Qminus.
```

Orient each candidate so its elliptic projection is `Qminus`.  If
`phi` is a function whose divisor is the candidate minus `3(P-O)`,
then equality with `3D9` in the generalized Jacobian is equivalent to

```text
rho=phi(Qplus)/phi(Qminus)=1.
```

The calculation uses the Miller function

```text
f_3,P=(Y-4*x-6)*(Y-3)/((x-3)*(x-6))
```

and the line through the two candidate points.  The results are

```text
q                 line Y=m*x+b   rho   conclusion
x^2+x+4           (m,b)=(3,6)     1    equals 3D9
x^2+2*x+3         (m,b)=(1,4)     4    independent
x^2+3*x+3         (m,b)=(0,3)     2    independent.
```

Here `{1,2,4}=mu_3(F_7)` is the 3-torsion in the split torus of the
generalized Jacobian.  The ratios 2 and 4 prove that the latter two
classes are not in the cyclic group generated by `D9`.  Prime-to-7
torsion injects into the Neron special fiber, so their characteristic-zero
Hensel lifts remain independent.  Together with the rational Weierstrass
class, a rational point on either branch would therefore give torsion
containing `[3,18]`.

## Bounded reconstruction

Run:

```bash
timeout 240 sage code/contact9_318_order3_boundary_lift.sage \
  --height-bound 200 --precision 16 \
  > data/contact9_318_order3_boundary_h200_p16.txt

timeout 180 magma code/contact9_318_order3_boundary_check.m \
  > data/contact9_318_order3_boundary_magma_p7_t4.txt
```

The Sage run exhausts all reduced `t=n/d` satisfying

```text
|n| <= 200, 1 <= d <= 200, gcd(n,d)=1,
7 does not divide d, t=4 mod 7, t != +/-1.
```

There are 6,146 such parameters.  Both independent branches were lifted
to `7^16`, for 12,292 lifts.  Coordinatewise rational reconstruction is
complete when numerator and denominator are each at most

```text
floor(sqrt(7^16/2)) = 4,076,329.
```

The result was

```text
tested_lifts=12292
exact_hit_count=0
```

At the central rational value `t=4`, Magma independently computes the
exact rational torsion group as `[18]`.

## Conclusion and next exact step

The earlier capacity test was genuine: the live nodal chart contains two
smooth local branches carrying an independent order-3 class.  It did not
produce a new rational `[3,18]` example in the stated box.

Further lifting of the same 7-adic discs is not the best next move.
Eliminate `h0,h1,h2,h3` from (1) over `Q(t)` to obtain the degree-40
unsigned `q`-cover, factor it over `Q`, and use the reductions

```text
(u,v)=(2,3), (3,3) mod 7
```

to select the components containing the independent branches.  Normalize
those components and compute their genera.  Rational-point methods should
then be chosen component by component.  The three rank-five solutions
with `q(6)=0` form separate node-touching charts and would require their
own blowups; they were not included in the bounded reconstruction.
