Platonov–Petrunin order-48/36 curves: recovered and settled

The flagged literature item (“if simple they’d beat Elkies’ cyclic-40 record”) is resolved. The models are printed in Platonov, Russian Math. Surveys 69:1 (2014) §6 (free full text on mathnet.ru):

f₄₈,₁ = (x−2)(x+2)(x⁴−10x²−3)    f₄₈,₂ = (x²+x+2)(x⁴+3x³+21x²−27x+18)    f₃₆ = (x+2)(3x²−6x+8)(3x³+6x²+3x+4)

Survey §7 proves all three non-simple over Q̅ (explicit zero-divisors in End⁰). Independent Magma verification confirms exact torsion [48]/[48]/[36], L-polys reducible 44/44, and identifies them as LMFDB 1764.a1 (factors: cond-21 [8]-curve × cond-84 [6]-curve — the very 6×8 gluing shape of Howe’s 2015 family), 5292.c2, and 4860.f1 — i.e. the table’s minimal [48]/[36] witnesses are P&P’s 2012 curves.

Method

Gluing lanes over field-bucketed pairs (LMFDB seeds + X₁(N) Tate-normal families, N ∈ {6,7,8,9,10,12}, runtime-verified Kubert parametrizations) via Genus2Elliptic2/3; funnel = J(Fp) invariants at 12 good odd primes → sound abelian-group compatibility test against the known set (odd part of a (2,2)-glue is exactly T₁odd × T₂odd, which sharpens the abort) → exact TorsionSubgroup only for survivors. Negative sweeps recorded: Richelot closures, isogeny-class sibling webs, bielliptic y²=ct(x²), factor-pattern gain hunt. Everything in product/ (code, data, logs).
