The order-31 realization (Z/31)

GPT 5.6 Sol noticed that a curve sitting in Edgar Costa's ModularAbelianSurfaces/olddata has a rational point of order 31 on its Jacobian. This PR contains the verification and everything that followed over three days: a new record, a corrected conjecture-landscape, a narrow-class obstruction theorem-shape, five exhaustive negative campaigns, seven newly reconstructed curves, and an infinitude census of all 72 realized torsion groups.

1 · The realization (new record)

Curve (newform 1830.2.a.q, level 1830 = 2·3·5·61, RM by ℚ(√2)):

y² + (−x² − x)·y = −839x⁶ + 2841x⁵ − 4587x⁴ + 4300x³ − 2466x² + 816x − 126

J(ℚ)tors = ℤ/31ℤ exactly, generator with Mumford coordinates u = x² − (21/20)x + 9/20, v = (101/200)x + 51/200 — invisible support (conjugate quadratic points; the curve itself appears to have no rational points). Geometrically simple (D4 certificate at p = 11); RM core {2} at 164 primes; Hecke-trace match to the newform at every checked prime.

This is the first explicit rational point of order 31 on an abelian surface over ℚ, and the first order-31 realization on a genus-2 Jacobian (previous explicit prime record: 29, Leprévost 1995; Elkies 2001–02 had only a 31-subgroup without rational generator; Platonov's 2014 survey lists primes through 29 only). The existence of an order-31 point on some inexplicit member of this isogeny class already follows from Alessandrì–Coppola's Theorem 4.2/4.3 with their level-1830 computation; what is new here is the explicit curve, the exact group ℤ/31ℤ, and the certificates. It confirms entry 31 of their Conjectures 4.5/4.6 (arXiv 2602.21047 v3 — v1/v2 excluded 31; the campaign initially mis-read the version history and the correction is recorded). The torsion order had been sitting, unnoticed, in Costa's public modav-torsion.out since 2024.

2 · Why the lab missed it (post-mortem)

Structural, not accidental: the target-ranking criteria scored candidates by split-Jacobian existence evidence (impossible for prime torsion ≥ 11) and by toolkit-expressibility (contact/halving/composition cannot express any prime beyond 7). ℤ/31 was named open exactly once (2026-07-17), then silently dropped. Modularity/RM was used only as contamination to screen out — never as a source — and every lab construction forces visible torsion, while this class is invisible. The curve's Jacobian has conductor 1830² > 2²⁰, structurally outside every database scan. Full diagnosis: notes/claude_z31_postmortem_2026_07_30.md.
