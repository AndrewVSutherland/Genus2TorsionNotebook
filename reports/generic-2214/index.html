Generic [2,2,14] realized: five genus-2 Jacobians over Q with torsion Z/2×Z/2×Z/14 and End(JQ̄) = Z

torsion_jac session 2026-07-23 · construction, sweep, and PARI gates ran on a box where Magma was not installed; exact verification in Magma once on a system with it installed.

Status before today: [2,2,14] (order 56) and [2,22] (order 44) were the only two groups in the realizations table whose geometrically simple witnesses were all RM (real multiplication) — and no torsion group containing (Z/2)²×Z/7 appears anywhere in the published literature for any endomorphism type (QM is excluded outright by Laga–Schembri–Shnidman–Voight; split Jacobians cannot carry it by Mazur — an elliptic factor with a rational 7-torsion point has torsion exactly Z/7). Today [2,2,14] has five generic witnesses.

The construction (three-root route on the contact-7 chart)

Work on the lab's contact-7 chart: h = 1 − (7/2)x + ax² + bx³, f = (h² + (x−1)⁷)/x² — a monic quintic whose Jacobian carries the marked class [(1, h(1)) − ∞] of order 7. Every rational root r of f satisfies 1−r = v² with

a + b(1−v²) = A(v),   A(v) = (2v⁵+4v⁴+6v³+8v²+10v+5) / (2(v+1)²)

— linear in (a,b). Two roots (parameters s,t) solve for (a,b) — the known rational two-root surface with visible Z/2×Z/14. A third root u exists iff the three points (1−v², A(v)), v ∈ {s,t,u}, are collinear on a rational plane quintic — a residual cubic condition R(s,t,u) = 0 (S₃-symmetric, tridegree (3,3,3), quadratic in e₃). Factor type [1,1,1,2] then forces 2-rank 3, so the torsion contains (Z/2)³×Z/7 = [2,2,14]. Unlike the 3-torsion "+3" covering condition studied on this chart in July (degree-40 cover, genus ≥ 15), this is a degree-3 cover of a rational surface — and it is point-rich: a height-32 sweep found 16 hits forming 6 distinct curves.

Where this leaves [2,22]

Still open, with a much sharper map (see notes/claude_generic_222_2214_plan_2026_07_23.md): every known witness has its odd torsion class at infinity (CF orders 11/7 — including both RM witnesses), so the target is the surface {[1,1,2,2]-shape ∩ D∞-order-11}, which provably has rational points. Measured dead today: all easy 2-upgrades of Flynn's family (and the repo's "Daowsud–Schmidt family" was proved to be Flynn's — Gt/4(x+1) = Ft(x); the corrected arXiv-v2 family is genuinely new and now transcribed). Live routes: the genus-0 quadratic-factor component of Flynn (parametrization running), the BLP2009 resultant surface (its broken table row C₄ was uniquely repaired, yielding a second explicit [2,22] curve — RM(√5) again, but the surface itself is provably not RM-forced), CF-backward construction anchored at the RM witness, and Chabauty closure of the branch-point family's genus-3 cover.
