The ranked ten

  1. Contact-7 SPLITALL → [2,2,2,14], order 112, a new record new this review

    The contact-7 chart's whole rational-root structure is governed by three universal linear relations. With h = 1 − (7/2)x + a x² + b x³ and f = (h² + (x−1)⁷)/x², a rational root r forces r = 1 − v², and the five vi are the roots of

    Q(v) = (v+1)² (c₄v² + c₀) + v⁵ − v³ − v²/2 ,   c₄ = b+2,  c₀ = 5/2 − (a+b)

    so, identically in (a,b): e₂ + 2e₁ + 1 = 0, e₄ + 2e₅ = 0 (i.e. Σ1/vi = −2), 2e₃ − 2e₁ − 2e₅ − 1 = 0. The ladder is then explicit — and it is the same ladder that gave Elkies his order-80 record on contact-5:

    rational vifactor type2-ranktorsionstatus
    1[1,4]1[14]realized
    2[1,1,3]2[2,14]realized (rational surface)
    3[1,1,1,2]3[2,2,14]realized 2026-07-23, five End=Z curves
    5[1,1,1,1,1]4[2,2,2,14], order 112never derived, never searched

    Four rational roots force the fifth, so the order-112 locus is the double cover of the three-root surface given by "the residual quadratic splits". The repo names this only as a one-line "bonus watch".

    Done today. A two-tier C harvester (code/claude_c7_harvest.c) reproduces the project's 16 recorded three-root hits at H = 40 in 0.85 s (their sweep was H ≤ 32 in gp), and at H = 600 has already produced 11 distinct triples, 5 of them new. All five verify in Magma with factor type [1,1,1,2], marked class of order exactly 7, TorsionSubgroup exactly [2,2,14], and a scattering real-subfield-disc signature (the End=Z signature) — against the control (−3,−3/4,−3/5), which returns the constant {2} of the known RM(√2) curve:

    (-511/61, -511/625, -1/2)     (-165/41, -33/16, -165/289)
(-164/297, -1/2, 164/361)     (-17/50, -34/189, 34/121)

    Split-all: zero so far, as expected — a square condition cuts a measure-zero subset, so brute force is the wrong instrument. No local obstruction: split-all configurations exist mod 17, 23, 29, 31, 37, 41, 43, 47, 53, 59, with counts matching the naive p²/120 heuristic.

    Next moves. (i) Fit low-degree rational curves through the growing point set on R(s,t,u)=0 — every triple found, old and new, has the common-numerator form vi = c/ki (11/11), so a rational curve is very likely, and one turns five sporadic curves into an infinite generic [2,2,14] family. (ii) Restrict the split-all square condition to each such curve: a needle-in-a-surface search becomes a genus-0/1 problem decided in minutes. (iii) Same surface, other rungs: halve a rational Weierstrass class ([2,2,28], also order 112) and add an independent cubic 3-contact (order 168).

  2. Generic [2,22]: route B2′ on a directly parametrized [1,1,2,2] ansatz, and route B4 (Galois-stable Richelot)

    The last RM flag. The 07-23/24 work closed the obvious doors properly (every parametrized order-11 family is 2-upgrade-locked; the DB 11-slice was counted exhaustively). Two live routes, plus one never run.

    B2′. BLP's ansatz is y² = R(x)² − 4c²S(x)², R = x³−x²+ax+b, S = x²+d, i.e. a product of two cubics. The [1,1,2,2] shape (the shape both known [2,22] curves have) is "each cubic acquires a rational root" — and that condition is linear: given (c,d,r₁,r₂), the two root equations solve for (a,b). So the 2-structure is free, and only ord(D∞) = 11 has to be imposed — testable in microseconds by the polynomial-continued-fraction machinery the repo already has and has validated. Two rational anchors are in hand (BLP's corrected row C4 with r₁ = 9, r₂ = −21; and 19044.h.2). No code has ever been written. This is the concrete form of the project's recorded winning pattern: build the 2-structure in from the start.

    B4, and a recorded blocker that is wrong. new this review Commit 34cfb59 blocks Richelot walks because "[22]/[14] seeds are 2-rank 1, so they have no rational (2,2)-kernels". A Richelot kernel need only be Galois-stable, not pointwise rational. Verified in Magma: a curve with TwoTorsionSubgroup = [2] can have a Richelot isogeny over Q (y² = (x²+x+1)(x⁴−4x²+2)). Measured on a 121-curve sample of 2-rank-1 curves whose quartic cofactor has Galois group in D₄: 88 neighbours, 1 with higher 2-rank — about one in ninety raises the 2-rank. Flynn's (6,2) component supplies infinitely many [2,4]-type members (its double cover is the rank-1 conductor-92 curve y² = x³+3x²+2x+1), and a (2,2)-isogeny preserves End⁰ and the odd part, so genericity and the 11 both survive. First computation: the genus/rank of the resolvent-cubic cover over that component.

    correction Richelot-walking the RM anchors cannot produce a generic witness — End⁰ is an isogeny invariant, so every neighbour is again RM(√5). process The two Magma jobs constituting route B1 (claude_v7_cfbackward.m, claude_v7_stratum2b.m) have been hung for 34 hours at ~99% CPU with no output, both with SetMemoryLimit(24e9) against the lab's 3 GB convention, occupying 2 of the 3 permitted Magma slots. Kill them by PID (and sweep for detached magma.exe children); replace the symbolic approach with the mod-p interpolation that Addendum 6 already designs.

  3. [6,12], order 72 — finish Prym–Chabauty

    The most decidable target: it either produces the curve or closes the lane, and both are publishable. rank J(E8) = 4 = genus so classical Chabauty fails by exactly one, but rank(Prym) = 1 < 2 is confirmed by a committed self-contained verifier, and elliptic Chabauty is proved unavailable. What blocks it is mechanical: the committed Coleman sweep covers 15 of 42 residue disks, has no TOTAL ZEROS line, and is numerically unsound (O(37⁴) annihilator coefficients coerced into a precision-20 parent; hard-coded 37^mv; Tuitman's inf flag discarded, so zeros in infinity disks are tested on the wrong fiber) — the bundle is formally withdrawn. And the Abel–Prym Mordell–Weil sieve has no code at all.

  4. Run the database queries that were never run — cheapest decisive item on the board

    The extended-database group list is inherited, not measured: the 2026-07-22 census only refined witnesses for groups already on the collaborators' hand-curated Table 1 (torsion_subgroup IN (…)). No torsion histogram over the 6.2M-curve Booker–Sutherland collection exists, so "absent from the DB" is an assumption for every open target except the 11-slice.

    SELECT torsion_subgroup, count(*) FROM g2c_curves_new WHERE is_simple_geom GROUP BY 1;
SELECT torsion_subgroup, torsion_order, label, cond, geom_end_alg FROM g2c_curves_new
  WHERE is_simple_geom ORDER BY torsion_order DESC LIMIT 50;
SELECT geom_end_alg, torsion_subgroup, count(*) FROM g2c_curves_new
  WHERE is_simple_geom AND geom_end_alg <> 'Q' GROUP BY 1,2;
SELECT class, array_agg(DISTINCT torsion_subgroup), max(torsion_order) FROM g2c_curves_new
  GROUP BY class HAVING count(DISTINCT torsion_subgroup) > 1;

    Two supporting facts: a "DB says RM-only" verdict was overturned within a week for [2,2,14] (small conductor is populated by factories that force RM — both RM witnesses have square conductor, 138² and 390²); and conductor is an isogeny invariant, so if the DB is conductor-complete then no 2-power isogeny walk from a DB seed can produce a group absent from the histogram — which retro-explains all eleven zero-hit Richelot campaigns.

  5. The split-realized targets nobody has ever touched: [63], [45], [7,7], [5,10] — and Howe's f70 for [35]

    HLP 2000 Table 1 lists twenty groups realized on split Jacobians, i.e. their moduli have honest rational points and only simplicity is missing. Four have literally no note, no code, no log. Z/63 is the only unrealized target with an exact split realization (a single curve), and a simple Z/63 would be a new cyclic record (currently Elkies' [40]); recovering its equation from HLP Proposition 4 is step one, and the [6,12] note is the worked template.

    A five-minute experiment with a large payoff: the literature intake already holds, CF-verified, Howe's sextic f70 with ord(D∞) = 35 — an explicit genus-2 curve with a rational class of exact order 35, while Z/35 is under active attack. Nobody has run TorsionSubgroup and a simplicity certificate on it.

    Never attempted anywhere: simultaneous contact-6 × contact-7 for [42] and contact-7 × contact-9 for [63]. Only 5×6 (successful), 5×5 (proved empty), 7+5 and 9+5 have ever been formulated, and the 5×6 derivation is a complete template.

  6. [8,8], order 64 — write the symbolic conic lift layer

    The most-named unexecuted computation in the repo, and the healthiest local picture of any open target (containment 6/98 at q=7, 39/589 at q=11, 106/1097 at q=13, no obstruction through p=43). The genus gate is passed. Scanning is explicitly the wrong move: 775 bases, 1,020 members, 32 gate-passes, all exactly [4,4]. The route is mapped and never written — Kummer coordinates of Di over Q(s,t,v) via Nicholls §5.9.2, then the even-degree x−T presieve Res(lj, uDi) square plus common-λ compatibility, then the conic's Hasse obstruction primes.

  7. [2,2,16] — the seven twisted norm surfaces (and the unrecognized [2,2,18] gap)

    The seven twisted norm surfaces Yt on M₁(8,2,2) have never been enumerated, and that chart is the only configuration that can give exact [2,2,16] (chart 2 can only overshoot to [2,2,2,16]); the note says the sweep takes minutes. Separately, the symbolic parity audit of the uniform 1/5 delta ceiling on chart 2 could kill an entire chart in one stroke.

    [2,2,18] (order 72) is a genuine gap on no ranked list and in no note. new this review The obvious route is dead: on the contact-9 chart the two-rational-root locus {ψ(v₁) = ψ(v₂)}, with ψ(v) = (v⁵+4v⁴+10v³+(215/16)v²+(35/4)v+35/16)/(v+1)⁴, is an irreducible curve of genus 6 (Magma). So contact-9 gives no [2,18] family, a fortiori no [2,2,18]; that target needs a different order-9 source.

  8. [3,12], order 36 — the smallest open group

    Essential correction on record: rational points of the carrier S12 do not give [3,12] — the extra 3-class is rational iff M is additionally a square, so the true carrier is the étale double cover m² = M, and M is not constant along the degree-3 fibers. Two named, never-run computations: the Mordell–Weil sieve on both genus-2 quotients (MW proved Z/2 × Z × Z with explicit generators; needed because rank = genus = 2 blocks Chabauty), and prove-or-refute the M square-class dichotomy {1, −3}. Two stale claims to avoid: BFS-√3 is closed for [3,12] by the same μ₃ argument that closed [2,6,6], and "both order-12 charts are dead at 5 and 7" is wrong (p = 7 is alive on M(12)).

  9. Beyond order 96: [2,2,2,24]/[2,2,4,12] (192), [2,2,4,8] (128), and the multigrade infinitude question

    That component of M(2,2,2,12) is the classical multigrade variety ΣXi² = ΣBj², ΣXi⁴ = ΣBj⁴ (three terms vs two), on which both square conditions hold identically. Exactly three curves are known; the census is complete to X₁,X₂ ≤ 250,000, X₃ ≤ 10⁷. The literature dig on classical parametric multigrade families (Gloden; Choudhry) has never been done, and one nondegenerate family gives infinitely many order-96 curves — which is what every order-192 route needs (three lottery tickets is not a program). The ten pair-type components are unexplored. For [2,2,2,24], eleven locally-viable families survive (F1 F6 N3 N4 N11 O4 O9 R3 R7 S3 W2), none given the component-complete treatment that closed the others.

    correction The [2,2,2,12] quintic-chart enumerator's "unconditional kill for all gcd-1 tuples a<b<c<d ≤ 2000" is vacuous: each known curve's admissible normalizations into that chart have max entry 4.4×10⁷ … 4.5×10¹¹, so the box is ~2×10⁴ times too small even for the smallest known solution. The enumerator was never validated on a positive control and its source (code/tor22212.c) does not exist in the repo despite four notes calling it "ready, never run at scale".

  10. [2,24], order 48 — decide it, and touch the route nobody has touched

    The conspicuous gap between [2,22] and [2,26], attacked by six independent programs, all negative, locally alive on every chart tested. The HLP [2,24] split family (Forum Math. 12 §3.4) has never been touched — nothing in notes/, code/, data/ or results/ refers to it; deforming it off the split locus is the standard play. And the commit-6f7c60c "wall" is not a theorem — the note itself heads that section "Conjecture (theorem-shaped, next to prove)": 6,420-point census, 40 points probed for norm/signature, only 12 for the actual mechanism, and the probe scripts already do the right thing — only their loop bounds stop them from covering all 389 both-square points. Do not restart the "25 unfitted off-main halving points" line: it was overturned the day after it was proposed.

Honourable mentions

  • [4,16] is now a closure story: the per-R elliptic funnel is complete over all 6,086 fibers to w-height 10⁶ with zero exponent-16 points, 627 unconditional non-existence theorems are in hand, and there is no local obstruction. One named computation remains — the rank of the 2-dimensional Prym of the genus-3 plane quartic CAX on the last open ELS fiber R = −8.
  • [2,6,6]: restrict the 3-contact cover to the rational curves C2, C3 and compute the cover curve's genus/rank — spec'd as "highest value, cheap", never run.
  • (3,3)-isogeny descent (Bruin–Flynn–Testa) is not implemented anywhere and is the one isogeny direction that could add something: the 3-part of the three order-96 curves is provably untouched by the 2-power Richelot graph.
  • The CF/Pell exact-order screen costs microseconds per curve and has been pointed at only three lanes. Cheap decisive experiment: test whether "every witness carries its odd class at infinity" (verified only for CF orders 11 and 7) extends across the whole [2,2n] chain.
  • The multigrade phenomenon has never been sought for any other target, though [2,2,2,10] is governed by the Clebsch–Klein cubic and the [2,2,4,4] witness x(x+36²)(x+57²)(x+64²)(x+132²) has a conspicuous pattern (64²−57² = 7·11², 64²−36² = 7·20², 132²−57² = 7·45², 132²−36² = 7·48²).
