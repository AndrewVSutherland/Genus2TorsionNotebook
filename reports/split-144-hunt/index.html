2. New positive results

2.1 An infinite family of [8,8] split Jacobians

A class-(b) curve glued to itself along the conjugation anti-isometry is legitimate (Aut(E) = ±1 acts trivially on E[2]), and the halving condition becomes α = x(g₁)−erat ≡ 1 or disc mod squares. For N=8 the condition curve is the conic α ≡ t(1−t) mod squares, giving the family

t = a²/(a²+b²) on the Kubert X₁(8) chart  ⇒  J(Q)tors = [8,8] (all 27 instances of height ≤ 120 verified exactly)

[8,8] previously had only isolated witnesses.

The trace sieve revealed that the high-torsion families pair up inside isogeny classes: every Et2,6-curve has an X₁(12)-partner, [2,8]-curves have [8]-partners, X₁(10)-curves pair with each other. A Cremona-database sweep (conductor ≤ 20000) found the minimal instances:

The analytic gluing pipeline (analytic_glue.m: period lattices → Lagrangian graph enlargement → Frobenius symplectic basis → τ → Rosenhain → Igusa–Clebsch) finds, for each pair, exactly 8 (N=5) resp. 12 (N=7) anti-isometries whose glued surface has real invariants — a precision-stability test showed these detect definability over R (the M's commuting with complex conjugation on the period lattices), and most of them are genuinely irrational, i.e. not defined over Q. This matches the representation theory: for classes with only 2-power/3-power isogenies, EndGal(E[N]) is scalars, so the only Q-rational anti-isometries are the isogeny-induced ψ = c·φ|E[N] — and by the Kani criterion in the form N(a²+mb²) = □ those are degenerate for (N,m) = (7,6), (7,3), (5,4), (5,9), (5,11), but never degenerate for (5,6), (5,14), (7,10) (p-adic descents). The 90c pair ([12]×[2,6]) is 6-isogenous — the (5,6) window — so its two isogeny-induced gluings ψ = ±2φ|E[5] should be nondegenerate Jacobians over Q with torsion ⊇ [2,6,12]; the strict high-precision identification of these two among the 8 real-invariant candidates was running at session close.

The remaining bottleneck

Two steps remain: pinning the Q-rational members of each real-invariant family (strict rationality at precision ≥ twice the invariant height), then Mestre reconstruction. See the addendum for where this stood at session close.

  • The order-144 record itself is closest via [2,6,12] at class 90c — the surface is constructed and certified analytically; only the curve model is pending.

Addendum: how the isogeny-gluing route closed

A precision-stability test settled the recognition question: the "rational" invariants of the 8/12 real-invariant anti-isometries track the working precision — they are real but irrational, i.e. those gluings are defined over R, not Q (the M's commute with complex conjugation on the period lattices; genuine Q-rationality additionally needs Galois equivariance). For classes whose members connect by 2-power isogenies, EndGal(E[5]) is scalars, so the only Q-rational anti-isometries would be isogeny-induced — and a degree sweep over all Cremona classes to conductor 3·10⁵ shows every same-class pair with torsion product ≥ 96 connects by degree exactly 2, which is never anti-isometry-compatible (2·k² ≠ ±1 mod 5, ∉ {3,5,6} mod 7). The golden windows (5,6), (5,24), (7,10) are provably nondegenerate but require a 3- or 5-isogeny step that destroys the torsion the product needs. Conclusion: the (N,N)-gluing route to order ≥ 96 split torsion is obstructed at every accessible instance by the three-way tension between anti-isometry existence (c²m ≡ −1 mod N), Kani nondegeneracy (N(a²+mb²) = □ insoluble), and torsion preservation along the connecting isogeny. The classical (2,2)/(3,3)-machineries — whose complete condition-collapse theory this session derived and validated — remain the only constructors, and their frontier is exactly where [2,2,6,6] and [12,12] sit.
