The sigma-surfaces: universal deck sections, and the deck-confinement of [2,2,6,6]

The one attack surface left open by the 08-13 session was the family of five non-identity sigma-surfaces: for a (2,2)-gluing of E26(t)×E26(u) (both factors from the universal [2,6]-torsion family), the glued Jacobian has torsion ≥ [2,2,6,6] (order 144, beating the current split record 128) iff three explicit square conditions hold, one system per root-matching permutation σ in S₃. The σ=id system was closed on 08-13; the five others had 231–388 two-of-three points each at height 40 and had never been fibered. This session fibered all five — and found the reason the hunt keeps coming up empty.

1. Headline: every sigma-system has a universal section, and it is a deck graph

Factoring the j-equality correspondence of the family (deck_derive.m) gives, besides the diagonal, exactly five Möbius components and six (3,3)-components. The Möbius maps biject with the non-identity permutations (deck_sigma_match.m): each sigma-system is satisfied identically, over Q(u), by exactly one deck map:

This overturns the plan’s premise that the non-id surfaces are section-less torsor fibrations: they are elliptic surfaces with universal sections — but the sections consist entirely of deck pairs (j(E26(t)) = j(E26(u))), for which the “gluing” degenerates to a self-gluing (ruled out on 08-13). The 144-locus is nonempty on every surface; it is just, as far as every probe of this session can see, exactly equal to the deck locus.

3. The special curves and the p=3 wall

The scanner’s richest signals were rational curves on which one condition is identically square, collapsing the full system to a genus-≤1 problem in u alone. Exact simplification revealed the scanner’s deg-2 candidates to be Möbius curves in disguise; per surface they are the deck section plus a handful of special Möbius curves: t = u±4, (6u−9)/u, −9/(u−6) on (13); (21u−9)/(5u−9) on (123); (5u+11)/(u−1) on (132). Each reduces to one of just three quartics, e.g. t=u+4 gives

W² = −U⁴ − 4U³ − 118U² − 228U − 4209,    t=u−4 gives W² = −15U⁴ − 34U² − 15,

and all three quartics are locally insoluble at p = 3 (lane_2266_line_close.m) — the special curves are unconditionally empty. Note the fine structure: the ambient surfaces are everywhere-locally-soluble, yet every stratum on which the 144-condition “almost” holds is 3-adically obstructed; p=3 is exactly the degeneration prime of the E26 family (t = ±3 are excluded parameters). The obstruction lives on the boundary strata, not on the surfaces.

5. Where this leaves order 144

  • Closed today: the five sigma-surface fibrations (deck-section multiples + per-fiber MW + 48-curve sweeps), the nine special Möbius curves (p=3), the within-family 5/7-congruence route (empty sieve), and the twisted diagonals (now a theorem).
  • The structural verdict: the [2,2,6,6] locus on the E26×E26 chart is everywhere-locally-soluble but deck-confined in every stratum probed — genuinely thin, not locally obstructed. A genuine point, if one exists, lies on none of the identified rational curves and on no fiber reachable by height-150 joins + rank-≤2 coefficient boxes.
  • Still open elsewhere: the un-fibered (3,3)-gluing routes of the gap classification (§5 of product/split_torsion_table.md), order-144 via [2,6,12]/[12,12] on other charts, and the deg-(3,3) deck correspondences (non-rational sections) which were not exploited.
