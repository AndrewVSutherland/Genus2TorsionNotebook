All six Weierstrass x-coordinates are perfect squares, so the full rational 2-torsion (Z/2)⁴ ⊂ J[2] is visible at a glance; among the square roots, 218²−120² = 182² and 241²−120² = 209² (Pythagorean triples), and 218²−143² = 3·95², 266²−241² = 3·65² feed the 4- and 3-parts of the Z/12 factor.

This is not a remodel: the new curve has different G2 invariants (not a twist of the old witness) and its Euler factors differ at the good prime 37 (not Q-isogenous); it is absent from the old curve's Richelot-BFS tree. It was therefore verified from scratch:
