The reopening

Generic [2,22] is back on the table, at #1. The B2' note hypothesised

that the 2-rank-2 sublocus might be Humbert-forced, which would have explained

the RM-only pattern and closed the target. It is false: `Sigma'' =

{ord(D_inf)=11} in the [1,1,2,2] chart is not contained in H_5`. About

95% of Sigma'' lies off H_5 (measured at p = 61,101,151,199,251 with

a Krull dimension argument that rules out a vertical component), and

Sigma'' ∩ H_5 is a curve, on which both known witnesses and all 17 tuples

of the height-60 sweep lie.

1. A tool correction that touches everything

The lab's RM prefilter — *"real-subfield-disc census constant = RM, scattering

= End = Z"* — is wrong in the second direction. At a prime inert in the

field of definition of the RM, a1 = Tr(a_p) = 0 exactly, so

n_p = a1^2-4(a2-2p) = 8p-4a2 and its squarefree core is junk varying with p;

such primes have density 1/2. Consequently the screen scatters on **100% of

geometrically-RM curves whose RM is not defined over Q**.

Measured over all 4990 LMFDB RM curves: naive screen wrong on 3116/3116

with is_gl2_type = false, right on 1864/1874 with is_gl2_type = true.

Independently reproduced by the orchestrator on 12500.b.50000.1

(code/claude_rmscreen_verify.m): 55% of primes have a1 = 0, the naive census

returns 23 cores and the verdict "End = Z" — wrong — while the repair returns

the single core {5} and correctly names RM(sqrt5).

The repair is one line: count only primes with a1 != 0 and n_p neither

0 nor a perfect square. Right on 116/116 of the gold-standard set; 481/481

End = Z controls still scatter.

Nothing is retracted. The direction used to claim RM (constant => RM) is

sound, so "all 17 [2,22] tuples are RM(sqrt5)" stands. Every End = Z

claim rests on the strict two-prime Frobenius certificate, which is a theorem

and refuses an even chi rather than wrongly certifying it. But any note

saying "scattering => End = Z" must now be read as **"not yet certified either

way"**, and the cost is that we may have discarded generic curves as RM.

Separately: **every conductor computed with PARI genus2red was the odd part

only** (it is documented for p > 2 and silently drops the 2-part when its

factorisation matrix carries a [2,-1] sentinel). Five artifacts were affected

and all are corrected; the Magma replacement is validated 1113/1113 against

LMFDB in exactly the regime Magma disclaims. One inference died with it: the

[2,2,14] curves are not "odd non-squares, no GL2-type smell" — all six are

even, and the RM one is 152100 = 390^2, the GL2-type signature the note had

declared absent.

2. The ranked ten

1. Generic [2,22] — decide Sigma'' ∩ H_5, then sweep past height 60

Why first. It is the collaborator's priority, it is the last ^RM row in

the paper, and it is the only target whose geometry got better this week.

The Humbert hypothesis is refuted, so B2' is live; and we now know exactly where

the known witnesses sit.

The computation. Sigma'' ∩ H_5 is a curve carrying both known [2,22]

witnesses and all 17 height-60 tuples. Identify it and decide its rational

points. If it has finitely many and they are all accounted for, then **every

further [2,22] curve must lie off H_5, i.e. be generic** — which converts

the search from "find a generic one" into "find any new one at all".

Second computation. Extend the rational sweep beyond height 60. This is

the one place where a 192-core box is genuinely justified: H=60 cost 6.7 h on

192 cores and H=100 is 7.4x larger, i.e. ~300 h on claude-box's shared 32

vCPUs. The C sieve (code/claude_ov_b2p_scan.c) already shards.

Loose ends worth chasing. The core=3 residual (+7.8 points over the

conditioned control at p=61) is not resolved — a full H_12 component is

refuted at p=101 by >20 sigma, but a component defined over a quadratic field

would only be F_p-rational at half the primes, and two primes cannot exclude

that. And core=0 runs 3.1% against a 0.6% control: Sigma'' meets the

J ~ E^2 locus five times more often than chance.

2. Finish the [2,2,14] family theorem — the seven 2-divisibility covers

Status. THEOREM C+: 7898 of 7920 congruence classes (k mod 3960) of

E(Q) = <G> x <T> give members with J(Q)_tors exactly [2,2,14] and

End(J_Qbar) = Z; every genuine member with |k| <= 40 is certified. Eight

classes can never be settled by any reduction argument (the degenerate points

k = 0, ±1, ±2 mod L); enlarging the prime pool shrinks the excluded set but

never empties it.

What remains is one named computation: the seven 2-divisibility covers.

Writing f = (x-r1)(x-r2)(x-r3)q(x) with r_i = 1-s^2, 1-t^2, 1-u^2 and

deciding, over the function field, that no nontrivial 2-torsion class is

divisible would upgrade "7898/7920 classes" to "every nondegenerate member"

— a clean theorem, and the first family theorem for any group containing

(Z/2)^2 x Z/7.

Cheaper wins alongside: close the 12 prime-pool-limited classes with

PMAX:=40000 LTARGET:=3960, and split the two k = 1980 classes at

LTARGET:=7920.

3. [2,2,16] — build the 2-cover Z, and sample along Y_0(Q)

Why it moved up. The chart is now fully understood and the criterion is in

closed form: four explicit polynomial square conditions with no x-T module

and no number field (g12 = sq, g13 = sq, uv(u+v+1) = t^2,

lam0*(P ± 2st) = sq, with lam0 = (u-1)(v-1)(u+v+2) identical across all four

condition sets). The "seven unswept surfaces" turned out to be two

S_3-inequivalent ones, and both are now swept to H = 2000

(3.1e13 quadruples): 15 points, only six distinct curves, all with torsion

exactly [2,2,8] and none of their 16 order-8 classes 2-divisible.

Crucially: there is no local obstruction — the full halving condition is

soluble mod every prime 5 <= p <= 113 at exactly the predicted density 3/4.

So no finite-prime argument can ever close chart 1; if it is empty the reason is

global (Brauer–Manin / Sha on the 2-cover).

The two moves. (a) Build the 2-cover Z as a variety and decompose it —

the only thing that can end chart 1. (b) Better than any box sweep: three

d = -2 fibres of Y_0 have rank 2, so **Y_0(Q) is infinite and explicitly

parametrised** — sample eps along it rather than enumerating boxes.

4. [6,12] — decide Pr(Q)_tors and prove the saturation

Why it is cheap now. The Abel–Prym MW sieve was built and run over 26

primes. Lambda is cyclic over Q (W1 = 0, W3 = W2), so it is a

genuine one-generator sieve; g0 provably generates J(D)(Q); and the

inherited identification Pr ~ J(D) — on which rank Pr(Q) = 1 rests, and

which had never been checked — passes L_{E8} = L_{E4}·L_D at 19 of 19 primes.

E8 has no rational point over x = infinity and none of x-height <= 10^4.

The bound is d ≡ 0 mod 11200 unconditionally, or mod 3729600 if

Pr(Q)_tors = 0. Pr(Q)_tors is proved to be a 3-group of exponent 3 and

order dividing 9. **Deciding it is one computation and the bound improves 333x

with data already committed.**

Be clear about what this can and cannot do: an MW sieve returns a congruence,

never d = 0. Closing [6,12] still needs the precision-safe Coleman driver

(the committed bundle remains withdrawn: 15 of 42 disks, O(37^4) coefficients

coerced into precision 20, a hard-coded 37^mv, and a discarded infinity flag).

5. Order 112 off the c=2 slice — MW sieve on Cab_c

The record target is not dead, only harder than the old list implied. c=2 is

decided and was the only slice that could carry an infinite family, but

sporadic points elsewhere are not excluded, and the local evidence says thin

rather than obstructed.

The concrete move, named by the lane: a Mordell–Weil sieve on Cab_c,

slice by slice. Cab_c is elliptic of rank 1–3 with computable generators, and

"d1(P) and d2(P) both squares" is a quadratic condition on the MW lattice.

The 12 unfinished slices of the rank census are listed in the lane note (their

RankBounds enters an unconditional class-group proof; use GRH bounds).

Genus-4 Chabauty on the three-root fibre would be the frontal attack, but Magma

has no support and it would need a hand-built descent to elliptic Chabauty over

a number field.

6. [3,12] — the smallest open group, and untouched this round

Order 36; nothing in this campaign touched it, so its status is as the first

edition left it, with one correction. The true carrier is the **étale double

cover m^2 = M of S12**, not S12 itself (the extra 3-class is rational iff

M is additionally a square, and M is not constant along the degree-3 fibres).

Two named, never-run computations: the **Mordell–Weil sieve on both genus-2

quotients** (z=-5/3, z=-5/4), whose MW groups are proved Z/2 x Z x Z with

explicit generators — needed because rank = genus = 2 blocks Chabauty; and

prove or refute the M square-class dichotomy {1,-3}.

Avoid: the Bruin–Flynn–Shnidman sqrt3 route is closed for [3,12] by the

same mu_3 argument that closed [2,6,6], though the old note still lists it

as live.

Order 48, the conspicuous gap between [2,22] and [2,26], six negative

10. Attack W_N directly for the untouched split targets

Lane 8's structural result is the most reusable thing the campaign produced, and

it reframes a whole class of attacks. Because J[N] is finite étale, a torsion

locus is **the image of the rational points of a finite étale cover W_N of

M_2** — not a subvariety. "Is there a transverse component?" is therefore not

a deformation question at all; it is the arithmetic question *does W_N(Q) have

a point off the split locus*.

That is the right frame for the four split-realized targets nobody has cracked:

Z/63 (a single HLP curve with torsion exactly Z/63; a geometrically

simple one would be a new cyclic record, beating Z/40), Z/45, [7,7],

[5,10]. Current bounds: no Z/63 on the contact-7 chart to height 2000

(2.368e13 points) nor on contact-9 with |n| <= 3e5, d <= 3e4. And the census

question is settled in the target's favour — compatible absolutely simple

Weil classes exist at 53–60 of 61 primes p <= 293 for every target, with

Z/40 and Z/30 controls behaving identically, so there is **no local

obstruction**.

3. Method notes, updated

1. Instruction is not a mechanism. Nine lanes each told "at most one Magma

job" produced 38 concurrent processes at load 70. code/claude_magma_slot.sh

now enforces a global cap via flock on an inherited file descriptor, so the

slot frees even if the agent dies.

2. Measure before waiting. The [8,8] conic run was stopped at 25% after

/proc showed the critical path was ~31 h, not the 12.5 h that aggregate

throughput suggested — WaitForAllChildren waits for the slowest child. The

lane then reached an unconditional negative without it.

3. A prefilter is not a certificate. The RM screen (§1) was doctrine for

weeks. Only the strict two-prime Frobenius certificate ever justified an

End = Z claim.

4. Local health is not global existence — the campaign's most repeated

finding. [8,8], [2,2,16], order-112 and the W_N targets all have no

local obstruction at any tested prime and all are empty or thin in the

searched range. "Locally alive" should be treated as the absence of a cheap

kill, never as positive evidence.

5. Scope a theorem to what was proved. The order-112 result covers one

slice; it was tempting to write "the contact-7 chart cannot produce order

112", and that would have been false.
